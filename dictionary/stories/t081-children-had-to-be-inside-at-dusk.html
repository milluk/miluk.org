Children had to be inside at dusk

12 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

i-hɛ́·niyɛ́-k̓aʻ wí·an-ił-du an-íł-du-dúha'ya tli-iłdihí·mɛ qánu-kwi-á-licán·da yə-g̣atq̓áidiya,

The people of long ago did not want their children to play outside when it became evening,

Linked dictionary entries (7)

duhaya want · gatqai evening · hɛn far · íł they · k̯̓a' people, person · kwi demonstrative pronoun · qán·u outside

2

náim yuwút's máʻ-ditc x̣ú·t'łu·c g̣ɛ́ʻ-íksiᴍ.

because possibly some dangerous thing might mingle with them

Linked dictionary entries (4)

ma another, but, even if, really · ná·im because · x̣ú·ƛʼu·c monster, being · yuwu't's might, sometimes

3

x̣-wɛ́·n-ditc tłi-ił-kwí-an-dú·ha'ya qánu-alicání·da.

That is why they did not want them to play outside.

Linked dictionary entries (2)

kwi demonstrative pronoun · qán·u outside

4

yuwút's-x̣-ɛ́·cin kwí-paukʷdzə́-mɛ.

Perhaps a wild person of the woods might steal them.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

ɛcin forest being, wild thing · kwi demonstrative pronoun · pukwi enslave, to take as slaves · yuwu't's might, sometimes

5

x̣-wɛ́·n má·tsi-g̣atq̓áidiya mán-lix̯lí·mu tłi-hí·mɛ,

That's why as evening approached they took their hime then

Linked dictionary entries (5)

gatqai evening · himɛ child · lix̯lí·mu took in/ invite · mán already, directly · má·tsi just, merely

6

náim-ił·gu-s-ditc-álqsa.

for they feared various things.

Linked dictionary entries (2)

gú··s all, always · ná·im because

7

yuwút's-nú·sgíli g̣ɛ́ʻ-íksim.

A pitch-dress-ogress might mingle with them (at that time).

Linked dictionary entries (2)

nú·sgíli grass · yuwu't's might, sometimes

8

wɛ́n-kwi k̓úmts-tłi-hí·mɛ i-nu·sgíli-g̣ɛʻ-íksiᴍ.

The children'd die if a ogress got among them then.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

himɛ child · kumi out of breath · kwi demonstrative pronoun · nú·sgíli grass

9

wí-yuwút's i-du-łqwə́ʟ tła-g̣aháisdja,

And if the (evening sunset) sky were red,

Linked dictionary entries (2)

g̣áháis night, or nights · yuwu't's might, sometimes

10

kwí-wɛ́·n'nɛt íł-du-hís kwí-aɴ-qánu-wá·wa tłi-iłdihí·mɛ,

that's the reason why they also didn't have their children outside,

Linked dictionary entries (4)

his along, also, too · íł they · kwi demonstrative pronoun · qán·u outside

11

náim-tsa-du x̣ɛ́nwɛ́' ɛs dzə́nɛ i-łqwə́ʟ tłá-g̣a-háis.

because when the sky was red a disease was coming.

Linked dictionary entries (3)

dzɛnɛ come · ná·im because · xɛnwɛs sick, to be sick

12

wɛ́n-ditc tła-an-hí·mɛ qánú-álicaní·da ya-g̣atq̓áidiya.

Linked dictionary entries (4)
