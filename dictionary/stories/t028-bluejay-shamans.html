Bluejay shamans

19 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

ɣɛ'yɛ́ʻnɛ íl·áx̣q̓ain

Bluejay shamans

2

gwɛ́is gu·s-mín-du·-kwí-ałam-yúg̣wa.

There was a girl who was always picking myrtle nuts.

3

mín·t'ci tsu-kwí-x̣ɛ́nx̣ɛnu tłə-gwɛ́is,

Once the girl became ill,

4

łə́n·wi-kwi·-x̣ɛ́nx̣ɛnu

she became extremely ill.

5

íl·ax̣q̓ain-ł-hantł-g̣áłam·ida-idjá·u də-íł·áx̣-q̓ain.

We must get a shaman.

6

hídji tsa̯-k̓ɛ́l·ɛ íl·áx̣q̓ain tłə-ɣɛ'yɛ́n·ɛʻ.

I wonder where there is a shaman.

7

á·i łá·dza·tcil.

They say that bluejay is a good shaman.

8

tsú-wɛ·n á·yu-łá·dzita·m tłə-íl·ax̣q̓ain.

Very well then you go get him.

9

á·yú-djiʻ,

And so then indeed they went for the shaman.

10

tsú-x̣dzí·dzu·nəs tłə-gwɛ́is

Sure enough he came, and then he labored over the girl

11

hə́ cíct'cicílɛ·, dí·ła á·x̣ayał'í.

He/ It is my own poison-power that they are talking about.

12

tsú·ʻ dzí·dzɛ.

And so he doctored her.

13

łú·dədɛ·yɛ́·tcil

keep watch on him.

14

ha·-dú-t'smí·x̣wən-du kwə-ɣɛ'yɛ́n·ɛ.

That bluejay is tricky.

15

hɛi-má·tsi á·yú kwi·-má·tsi hwə́l·di,

And then sure enough he just flew up,

16

kwi·-dít̓im tłə-gwɛ́is

packing the girl on his back.

17

ha'ə́n·dja·-hwə́l·di.

He leaped (flew) through the smole hole (and away).

18

háhahahahaha ɛ́n·ɛ-'nəhú·misíyɛ

Ha/hahahahaha (laughter) she has become my very own wife

19

łá·ya-tłə-gwɛ́is

He stole the girl.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
