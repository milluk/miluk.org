Hadjíyasa, tłɛ́iɣəl, and others

90 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tłi-yá·x̣adja djinyá-dək̓a,

After they had moved the people to Ya<hatc,

2

anyá-ił-x̣-wɛ·n iłdətá·má·łis.

they no longer practised their own customs.

3

tłi-iłdɛ́·x̣-ta·má·łidjis má·tsiya íł-x̣-wɛn dətá·má·łis djíʻtłə-n̥tsłɛ́·nɛ́·-k̓a-də-tá·má·łis,

Their ways became the ways ( the customs ) of the moving people (the whites),

4

his-iłhídji x̣-wɛ́'niyɛ ił-dətá·má·łis.

they also began to practise such customs.

5

wí·-kwi·-tłá·wi tłɛ-x̣-tłɛ́iɣəl tłə-hadjíyasa,

6

tłə-dɛ-x̣-hú·mis də-x̣-hadái-'misitc,

with his (other) wife's money,

7

tit'sɛ́·wəs.

(Fanny was then) a girl just menstruating for the first time.

8

kwi·-tłá·wi tłɛ-x̣-tłɛ́iɣəl tła-áiwa tit'sɛ́·wəs tłə-hadjíyasa.

(m.tî<iâel bought hadji<yasa who was just a girl who had recently menstruated for the first time.

9

Then tî<iâel purchased hadji<yasa.

18

21
