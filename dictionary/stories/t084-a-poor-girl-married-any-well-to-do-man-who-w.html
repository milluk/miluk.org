A poor girl married any well-to-do man who wanted her

31 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tlə-kwí·'nɛ'wɛt'ł-k̓a i-du-x̣-k̓á kwi·-dú·ha'ya tłi-iłdəgwá·ya,

2

wí-wɛn-ił-du-íldwa,

3
