A mít̓î·din shaman's prayer

9 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

ɛ'nə́-x̣-k̓á dlúwi·n

It is not a person who is eating you.

2

x̣-mɛx̣ɛ́yɛn dlu'wí·n.

It is eagle who is eating you.

3

x̣-gugúm-nədlu wí·n.

It is raven who is eating you.

4

x̣-wa-'ás-nədlu'wí·n.

It is seagull who is eating you.

5

ɛ'nə́-x̣-k̓á-dlu'wí·n.

It is not a person who is eating you.

6

di·ł-k̓áʻ-ha·qʷdzú·n tłi-iłnəmá·ni-'ya·sədjadə-kwí·-łá.

The person who has left us has gone to our father.

7

g̣wá·n g̣á-hantł̯-k̓íl·ɛ·-dlú·g̣wa.

Up above there he will live well.

8

án-hántł dá·s-kwi-hɛ́udzət'sɛ́·nu.

He will not have a hard time any more.

9

tłí·-łmɛ-x̣-má·ni'yá·s hántl-kwíyɛ łú·dədá·ya.

Our father will look after him.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
