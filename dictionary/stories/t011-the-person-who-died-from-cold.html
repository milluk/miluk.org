The person who died from cold

38 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tłə-k̓áʻ x̣-g̣ɛ inɛis-tsáu

The person who died from cold

2

tsú-kwi-húwɛ' ɛ́tsəm-dək̓áʻ,

The people were going somewhere,

3

hí·mɛ-dəg̣a ·́ł kwí-huhúwɛ'ɛ́tsəm.

a number of children were going (too).

4

úma·t'łí

(Said one child,) Grandma

5

hís-wantł-ɛ́n ·ɛ́-łaʻ,

I want to go also,

6

dín·ɛ'yuwúdjɛ tsántł łá·-də́k̓aʻ.

to where the ka are going to the place of the inland people

7

ła'yáq̓ai'í

Go then

8

di·-tłə́-'łáha.

But wear this.

9

ú·̓ an-wúdú·há'ya.

Oh! I don't want it.

10

an-wántł-g̣ɛ́inɛ.

I will not get cold.

11

an·ántł-g̯ɛ-g̣ɛ́inɛ

So you will not get cold

12

his-nántł-kwə-nəmilt· síya·t̓is,

Now you put on your gloves anyway,

13

wí·-his-kwə-nəqɛ́ilusni.

and also your moccasins.

14

ɛ́·· úma·t'tłi

Oh grandma!

15

an-wú-u·ha'ya.

I don't want them.

16

an-wántł ɛx̯ɛ́·x̯.

I will not get cold.

17

kwi·-hántł-g̯ɛ-án-ɛx̯ɛ́·x

So you will not get cold

18

di·-nántł t'łáha-ɛt'ihái

you wear this blanket

19

ú· an-wúduha·'ya.

Oh I don't want to.

20

tsú-ła·-də́k̓aʻ,

The people went,

21

his-hídji-ay·ú·-ła,

he went along too,

22

alicá·nidá il-la'áyam.

he played as he went along.

23

wɛ́·n-ł'áyim-də́k̓aʻ.

That is the way the people went on.

24

qłímniyu'wiyɛ.

He got behind.

25

ánya ig̯ɛ́·k·.

He was no longer with them.

26

tsú-dji·-də́k̓aʻ də́n·ɛ'yuwúdjɛ.

The people reached the place of the inland people.

27

áɴ-dji.

He did not arrive.

28

tsú-hit'ci-k̓áʻ bí·nát's,

One of the people went back then,

29

hɛⁱ-má·tsi-ɛ́q kwí·-gik̓íl·i·du,

and sure enough he found him dead,

30

sk·ɛ́·nɛn x̣-g̣ɛinɛ́is.

stiff from the cold.

31

tsú-k̓wɛ́·n wusú·su tłə-də'úmna·t'łədja.

He returned with the news to his grandmother.

32

húʻ-an-da-x̯-su·dɛt,

Humph. I guess he was not so great,

33

náu-q̓áyau.

and that is why he died.

34

tɛ́·tc-úní'níya,

I tried to give him things to wear,

35

wí-an-dú·ha'ya.

and he did not want them.

36

an-tsántł ɛx̯-ɛ́·x̯.

He said he would not get cold.

37

an-dá-x̯-su·dɛt,

He could not have been so great,

38

náu-q̓áyau.

and so he died.
