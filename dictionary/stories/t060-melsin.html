Stories

Melsin

26 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tłə-mɛ́lsin wí-kwi-án-hu·t'súwa tłə-wáiqdí-k̓aʻ.

Melsin was bothering the waiqdí (village) woman.

Linked dictionary entries (3)

hú·t'súwa perpetrate tricks · k̯̓a' people, person · kwi demonstrative pronoun

2

wí·-kwi-tłɛ-x̣-ct'cɛ́·lí-də́hɛʟ tłə-kwí-q̓x̣á- d(th)ək̓wháná·s wɛ́·n-łə́n·ɛ́·x̣də tłə-mɛ́lsin.

So Burnt Face (Chief Jim Tyee) cut off the ears and nose of Melsin.

Linked dictionary entries (2)

qxa cut · kwi demonstrative pronoun

3

x̣-wɛ́·n-kwi-wá·tsan tłɛ-x̣-ct'cɛ́·lí-də́hɛʟ,

That is what Burnt Face did to him,

Linked dictionary entries (2)

kwi demonstrative pronoun · watsan do to, treat

4

náim-tłɛ-x̣-mɛ́lsin tlə-dədítc,

because Melsin was his relative,

Linked dictionary entry (1)

ná·im because

5

xᵘk̓wəndá·di.

(by) his mother's sister.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

xukwinda mother's sister

6

x̣-wɛ́·n-ditc kwi tłə-mɛ́lsin q̓x̣ɛ́ᵘ k̓ʷháná·sdə.

That is why he cut Melsin's ears.

Linked dictionary entries (3)

qxa cut · kwhanas ear · kwi demonstrative pronoun

7

wí-dá-qłimníyu wí·-tłə-dlú·-g̣wa'átc gú·s-míɴ tłə-tcictcíl·u-dədlə́l·wɛ,

After that he (Melsin) stayed all the time with the son of tcictcíllu,

Linked dictionary entries (3)

gú··s all, always · mín time · qlimniyu henceforth, after

8

kwí·-dú-didí·lúł.

(who) had him for his young man

Linked dictionary entry (1)

kwi demonstrative pronoun

9

adzú-dəgwá·-ya tcictcíl·u,

tcictcíl·u had two daughters,

Linked dictionary entries (2)

gwaya daughter · ya irrealis, used during the conditionel

10

tłə-dədłə́l·wɛ-dəkwɛ·nɛ́t'łə́mɛ.

his son's older sisters.

11

tłi-itcdəmitłgwə́la wí-kwi-x̯ ɛ́nx̯ ɛnu wɛ́n-kwi·-q̓áyáᵘ.

Their younger brother got sick ( got sick with something ) and died.

Linked dictionary entries (2)

kwi demonstrative pronoun · qayau dead

12

wí·-x̣-wɛ́-n ił-du-ɣá·lá·nu.

Then this is what they said.

13

gwá-x̯-tsá·-g̯ɛ x̣-mɛ́l-sin tłə-kw̯í·-k̓i·túwa,

They claimed that Melsin had put a poison-power into him,

Linked dictionary entries (2)

k̯̓i·túwa poison power · kwi demonstrative pronoun

14

x̣-wɛ́·n-kwi·-q̓áyaᵘ tłi-itcdəhɛ́t'łɛ.

and that was why their younger brother had died.

Linked dictionary entries (2)

kwi demonstrative pronoun · qayau dead

15

wí-tłɛ-x̣-húmɛ̯·k̓ɛ wí-tłgú·sitc-itc'áuct.

The two women asked him to go with them in a canoe.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

tlgus canoe

16

Linked dictionary entries (2)

tlbilya tip over · tlgus canoe

17

Linked dictionary entry (1)

gal numbers, many

18

sɛ́ʻl head

Linked dictionary entries (2)

21

máuqwts drown

24

Linked dictionary entries (3)

26
