Excessive sexuality caused loss of strength and beauty

21 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

wɛ́n-ił-du-íldwa.

This is what they would tell them (to young men).

2

án·ántł-du-má·tsi-g̣ɛ-x̣ín·i'yim.

You should not be on top constantly

3

i-nə-gú·s-mín-łu·-dú·ha'ya gu·s-mínɛhɛtshɛ́its,

If you desire copulating all the time,

4

wí-an·antl-hɛ́·niyɛ̯-k̓ɛ́-lɛ,

you will not be good (healthy) long,

5

n̥ts'ái-nántł ánya̯-k̓ɛ́·lɛ,

your back will become no good,

6

gu·s-mín-hantł-du-gwá-t'cidjɛ́·tc.

it will always be like (pulling) strings.

7

k̓xú·nam-nántł-nələ́g̣wət,

You will lose your red-headed-woodpecker-scalp-feathers

8

i-nantł-hágu·s-mín wɛ·n-wá'nu.

if you do it like that all the time.

9

áiwa-nantł-án níct'c idzí·mis,

You will not be many years (of age),

10

máʻ-nantł áɴ-dji yuxwúna·-n̥ts'ái.

before you will be no longer able to hold (use) your back.

11

wɛ́n-ditc kwə-nantł-áɴ-gu·s-min hɛ́its-nəlɛ́·t'c.

That's why you shouldn't be fond of copulating all the time.

12

hís-du-hí·mɛ-húmɛ̯·k̓ɛ ił-wɛ́n-íldwa.

They also told that to young women.

13

ya-áɴ-t'smí·x̣wn wí-an-k̓xú· nam-dələ́g̣wət.

If you'ren't a trickst you'lln't lose red-headed-wood

14

wí-yu-gú·s-mín dɛ́·miłdə-dú·ha'ya g̣wa·ní·m x̯ín·i'yim,

If you want your husband constantly (missionary) on top of you,

15

án·hɛ-niyɛ mán-aya·tsəm k̓xú·nam-dələ́g̣wət.

Not long before you'll be ugly and lose your head-feathers.

16

wɛ́n-his-tłə-dəgá·t's ánya-g̣wá·n-k̯ú·yim,

And that is why your breasts too will not stand erect,

17

g̣ɛdlɛ́·n-kwi-mílɛ·t̓i.

they will hang down.

18

ánya-djinu·djɛ k̯̓ɛ́·lɛ,

Your body will be good no more,

19

yɛ-hɛ́lt̓-ha gu·s-mín-łu·-dú·ha'ya.

if you have wanted too much of that all the time.

20

wɛ́·n-ditc tli-ił-áɴ-gu·s-mín wándi·da,

That is why they did not do it all the time,

21

náim-ił-kwi-álqsa.

because that was what they feared.
