Marriage negotiations

48 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

i-du-ɛ́ikit'cinú·-k̓a,

When people were friends,

2

wí-kwi-ni̯k̓ílgadí·yɛq̓,

and that one had a child,

3

wí-his-tłi-má·-ni̯k̓ílgidí·-yɛq̓,

and the other one had a child too,

4

wí-i-ił-g̣ɛ́ʻ-dji'níyam tłi-iłdi'ɛ́ikit'cídjɛ,

and then when they came to there to their friends,

5

wí-məsá-nihí·mɛ́·dɛ.

they both had children.

6

wí-hit'cí di'lút'l-d(th)ə̯k̓ílga,

The one had a boy child,

7

wí-tłu-má· wí-gwɛ̯'ɛ́k̓-də̯k̓ílga.

the other had a girl child.

8

wí-wɛ·n-tłí tlə-k̓á dí-1út'ł-di̯k̓ílga.

Then this was what the person with the boy baby said.

9

kwí·-hántł hu·msín·u dí·snəhí·mɛ.

Let our children be man and wife.

10

wɛ́·s-nantł-níya'mi.

I will give you this much.

11

wí-his-kwí-wɛn-dú·ha'ya,

If he wished it that way too,

12

wí-wá'nu,

he said,

13

á·yu-hantłí

Very well indeed

14

wí-áyu-g̣wálqʷts tłə̯-k̓ílga,

So then he paid for the (girl) child,

15

wí-áyu-hú·msin·u'wiyɛ tli-hí·mɛ.

and now the children were indeed man and wife.

16

x̣-wɛ́·n ditc-tłɛ̯-ɛ́·k̓íli·-k̓á hú·misín·u.

That is how the little people were married.

17

x̣-wɛ́n-ditc-du tlə-də'má·ni'yas yuwút's adzú-mit̓íyadis dáʻ-dlɛ́·g̣ɛq,

This is why the parents remained there (at there) perhaps two months,

18

tłə-dí'lút'ł-dəmá·ni'yas.

the parents of the boy.

19

hít'cu'wi'í·dɛ tli-hí·mɛ,

The children could be together,

20

wí-áyu-ah-ca·ní·da.

and they could play together indeed.

21

wɛ́·n-itc-du há·'wíyam.

That is how they grew up.

22

yuwút's tłətc-gwɛ́'ɛ̯k̓-də'ɛ́łɛ g̣ɛ́ʻ-dlú·g̣wa tłə-dí'lút'ł- da'ɛłɛ́·djintc.

Maybe the father of the girl stayed there at the place of the boy's parents (also).

23

yuwút's psínł-mit̓íyadis dá-iłhít'cú'wi dlɛ́·g̣ɛq.

They might stay together there perhaps for three months.

24

wɛ́n-x̣-wɛ́·n ha·'wíyam.

That is how they had them grow up.

25

wí-áyu sú·ltdzímɛu yu-kwí-hít'cú'wi ałicá·ní·da.

Indeed they fell in love with each other when they played

26

x̣-wɛ́·n-íldwa tłə-dɛx̣-ɛ́nɛ tłə-gwɛ́'ɛ̯k̓,

The mother of the girl would speak to her in this manner,

27

wí·-his-tłə-dí'lút'ł.

and likewise the boy (would be spoken to by his mother).

28

x̣-wɛ́·n íldwa tłə-dɛx-ɛ́nɛ.

This is how his mother spoke to him.

29

hú·mísna.

She is your wife.

30

i-nantł-hɛ́·wi wí-kwi-nə́hu·mis.

When you grow up she will be your wife.

31

wɛ́n-íldwa tłə-di̯k̓ílga

That is how she spoke to her child.

32

wí-a·'yu áɴ-djílt'si·də́mɛ,

Then indeed they would not be bashful towards one another,

33

náim-hit'cú'wi há·'wíyam.

because they had grown up together.

34

x̣-wɛ́n-ditc-du tłə-kwi-alicá·nu'wá·wa.

That is why they had them play together.

35

á·yú-itc mansú·ltdínu.

Indeed they already liked one another.

36

wí-an-ítcdjí·lt'sí·də́mɛⁿ,

They were not bashful towards one another,

37

i-hɛ́-hɛmíldju tłə-dəhú·mis,

when they delivered his wife to him,

38

náim-itc mit'sisín·u.

because they knew each other.

39

tsú-máʻ-wɛ·n-tłi-iłdəhú·misəsá·-ni'was.

That is the way such people got married.

40

i-du-hɛ́mildjiu tlɛ-hɛ́miltc,

When the delivered girl was brought to her husband,

41

yi-wə́si'í·dɛ tłə-də'ɛ́łɛ tłə-gwɛ́is,

and when the father of the girl was about to return home,

42

wí· hadái-'mis du-níya tłə-k̓á n̥dlə́lwɛldjɛ.

he gave some money to the person whose son it was.

43

wí-k̓wá·'niyada-du x̣-djí tłə-kwí-hadái'mis-níya tła-x̣-k̓á-nəgwáicdja.

Then he (that person) understood why the money was given him by the person whose daughter it was.

44

wí-má·tsi łín·wi-du-híya tłə-dəhadái'mis,

Now then indeed strongly he gave money

45

x-g̣ɛ́t-du-łín·wi g̣á·ł níya tłə-dəhadái'mis,

from that point there he gave considerable quantity of money

46

náim-du-k̓wa·'níyada'aya tłə-du-kwí-áɴ g̣ɛdjɛ́·nɛn-di'lúwɛ tlə-dəg̣wə́l·áqʷ tłə-dəgwá·ya.

because he realized that he had not been satisfied at heart with the price for his daughter.

47

á·yú-du k̯̓ɛ́·lɛ-x̣-wɛ́n,

But now indeed (when) it was made good that way,

48

tsú-du-t̓ə́ma-wə́s·i.

then he (the father of the girl) did go back home.
