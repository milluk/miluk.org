An Alsea shaman was forced to take back a pain-power

31 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tsú-hantł-mɛg̣ɛ́n-də́-k̓a tlə-alsí·yá-k̓a.

The Alsea people were going to have a (dream) dance.

2

tsú-hit'cú·nwiyɛ-də́-k̓a

The people came together,

3

wí-kwí·-tłə-mɛg̣ɛ́n-də́-k̓a.

and then the people danced.

4

tsú-há·p̓-łá·dzətam.

They went to fetch (a bucket of) water.

5

wí·-kwi·-tłə-hít'ci tlə-íl·áxq̓ain kwí·-kwi·-g̣álám tłu-xʷkwɛ́·l,

One (male Alsea) shaman took ( picked up the bucket) the bucket,

6

hɛⁱ-má·tsi wa'nú'itc,

and then he said,

7

x̣-wí·-kwi·-g̣ɛʻ-g̯íl·a də́k̓xáⁱ

Who put a pain-power in there

8

ɛ́dji

Come now

9

g̣álam̓

take it

10

x̣-wi-nántł kwə-nə-kwí·-g̣ɛʻ-g̯íl·a.

whoever you are who put it in there.

11

g̣á·ł-hí·mɛ-díu

There are many children here (who might be poisoned).

12

ɛ́dji

Come here

13

g̣álam-kwə-nək̓x̣á

take back your pain-power

14

wí·-i-nantł-áɴ-g̣álam,

If you will not take it back,

15

wí-an·ántł-hɛ·nłyɛ dlɛ́·-wɛ.

you will not live long.

16

á·yú-mɛ-x̣-wɛn.

Indeed then that was what he (the guilty Alsea shaman) did.

17

adzú-hum̯ɛ́·k̓ɛ g̣ɛ́ʻ-ła'áyam.

Two females (also Alseas) went to there (to him).

18

g̣álám-nantł kwɛ-nək̓xáⁱ

You must take back your pain-power

19

i-nantł-áɴ-g̣alam,

If you do not take it,

20

wí·-x̣-ɛ́n·ɛ-wantł-g̣alam.

I myself will take it (and then you will die).

21

á-yú-x̣-wɛ·n.

So that is the way it was.

22

g̣a-lám-tlə-də́k̓xaⁱ

He took back his pain-power.

23

wí·-tsa·kán'itc tłə-dək̓xáⁱ tlɛ-x̣-húm̯ɛ́·k̓ɛ-íl·áx̣q̓ain,

Then those two women shamans helped him with (bad ) his pain,

24

yú-kwí·-galam.

when he took it back.

25

náu-ił-du-álqsa.

They were fearful of it.

26

x-wɛ́·n-ił-du̯·-k̓íldwa x-wí tłə-kwi·-g̣ɛʻ-g̯íl·a tłə-k̓xáⁱ,

That's how they found out the one who had put the pain in there,

27

náim-yi-x̣-mí·t'ci-g̣alam.

because he got it back himself.

28

yə-áɴ-dji·-bíya wí-q̓ayáu-du.

Had he not taken it back he would have died.

29

x-wɛ́·n-ditc kwi-ił-kwi·-álqsa,

That is how they were fearful,

30

kwi-ildə́k̓xaⁱ yi-x-wɛ́·n x-má·-k̓á-k̯łá·wi.

when someone else saw their pain like that.

31

wi-x-má·-íl·áxq̓ain-du tsá·kan.

But then other shamans helped them (take it back).
