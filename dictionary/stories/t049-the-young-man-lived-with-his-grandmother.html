The young man lived with his grandmother

47 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

úmná·t'łətc-itc-də-dłɛ́·g̣ɛq tłə-dí·luł

The young man lived with his grandmother

2

wí·-kwí·-t'lháidzi·ya tłə-x̣-dí·luł.

The young man was making an elk fur blanket.

3

wi·ʻ-wɛ́n-tłí-tłə-dí·luł,

And now this is what the young man said,

4

ə́ⁿ·· yip-yihú'lai dəkwɛ́·nɛt'ł g̣ɛ́·-utsá-u

əəə I wish I could sleep with the sister of yipyibu/'lai!

5

hɛ́i̓ djí-də-nətłí,

He/i! what did you say,

6

dím·si

grandson?

7

má·-u-án-djí-tłí

Why I did not say anything!

8

ə́·· qá'u'wímdámi djí-nɛtłí

əə/ I heard you say something!

9

má·-ukl-án-djí·-tł̓í

But I did not say anything!

10

máʻ-g̣ɛ́ʻ-mitcmin·át'c tłə-dəx̣-úmná·t'łətc.

His grandmother just kept on asking him.

11

má·-u-án-djí·-tłí

But I did not say anything!

12

tsí'yukʷ-máʻ-mitcmin-át'c.

She just kept on asking him.

13

djíʻ-g̯ɛ́·-ɛnə-kwí-dú-ha'ya ha-k̓wá·ni'yada

Why do you want to know?

14

dɛ́·n-du-út.-tłí.

Well I did say it.

15

yipyibu'lái dəkwɛ́·nɛt'ł g̣ɛ́·-utsá·u.

I wish I could sleep there with the sister of yipyibu lai,

16

ə́··· k̓wá·ni'ya'dá·'u.

əəəə I know it.

17

yə-hántł-djí an·ántł djilt'sá·i.

When she comes you must not be bashful.

18

tsú·wi'ya-nántł.

You should go to bed with her.

19

hɛi-áyu kwi·-djíʻ-tłə-gwɛ́is.

And then indeed the girl came.

20

kwí·-á·yu tsú·wí'ya

He did go and lie down with her.

21

tsú-itctsú·m,

Now they lay down,

22

tsú-x̯índəsá·ma-tłə-x̣-dí·luł.

and then the young man got on top of her.

23

ú··. gasíya-wut'łátc.

Oh I almost have to urinate.

24

máʻ-g̣ɛ-dác·i,

Oh just keep pushing it further in,

25

dim·ísi

grandson!

26

tsú-bɛ́lx̣səm-tłə-dí·luł,

Now the young man was angry,

27

hɛ́i-x-kwə-tłə-də'umná·t'łətcíya

(discovering that) she was only his grandmother.

28

tsú-dúqʷsəm tłə-dí·luł,

The young man got up,

29

wɛ́n-g̯icdjɛ́-tɛyə́x̣ɛu.

and he went down below to the water.

30

án-hɛ́·niyɛ da-dlú·g̣wa,

He was not there long,

31

hɛi-má·tsi k̓áʻ-djíyan.

and then a person came downstream (in a canoe).

32

djí--nuk̓wɛ́·n

What is the news?

33

qłə́mniya k̓á-nantł-sg̣wí·dun.

The next person along will tell you.

34

án-hɛ́·niyɛ-mán-asu-má·-djíyan,

It was no long time before another was coming downstream,

35

ma·-dú-mant'ct.

and then he asked again.

36

ú··. qłə́mniyu-k̓á' nantł-sg̣wí·dun.

Oh. The next person along will tell you.

37

ma·-dú-x̣-wɛ́n tłə́tsi-ya,

In just the same manner they told him,

38

g̣ɛnt'cínsi-tłgú·s ts'ál·du.

five canoes passed by

39

djí-g̯ɛ ɛnə-há-mitcmínt'c

Why must you ask that?

40

yipyibu'lái tsa-háits-də'umná·t'łətc.

is only that yipyibu'la/i copulated with his grandmother.

41

wí·-tsu-t̓ə́ma-mɛ·'mɛ́·yu'itc tłi-itc-də'umná·t'łətc,

Now then they came to war upon him and his grandmother,

42

tsú·-tsu· tłə-də'umná·t'łətc.

and then they killed his grandmother.

43

wí·-djilt'sái tłə-dí·luł,

The young man was ashamed,

44

tłə-kwí·-wəntdzá·t̓a tlə-dəx̣-umná·t'łətc

because his grandmother had lied to him.

45

wí·-áiwa du·-x̣-k̓áʻ kwi·-hamáq̓ tłə-dət'lhái,

The people can still see her blanket,

46

wɛ́n-his-tłə-hu·'m̯ík̓ da-łəx̣ɛ́·x̣ qʷłáyu.

and also the old woman lying on her back on the rocks there.

47

g̣ɛ́-x̣-k̓áʻ-kwi·-hamáq̓ áiyúwa.

There the people still see her
