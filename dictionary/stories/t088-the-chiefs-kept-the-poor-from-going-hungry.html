Stories

The chiefs kept the poor from going hungry

7 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tłə-hɛthɛ́·dɛ gu·s-g̣ɛ́ndjuwi-du wálíx̣t tłə-hí·mɛ-k̓aʻ,

Everywhere in the village the chief sent the young people,

2

tcán·ɣá-k̓a g̣ɛ́nɛ́·tc-k̓a.

young men (and) young women.

3

kwi·-dú-kwi-g̣ɛ́ʻ-q̓wɛ́ʻn·yau ła'áit̓a tłə-kwí·'nɛ'wɛt'ł k̯̓ibíndjɛ.

They were the ones to take food ( poor people ) to the poor people.

4

á·yu-du-gú·s-q̓wə́n·'yá'wa.

Then indeed everyone had food.

5

gu·s-mín-du-x̣-wɛ́·n.

That is how it was always done.

6

łú·dədáya tłɛ̯-x̣-k̓íl·ɛ-k̓a tłə-kwí·'nɛ'wɛt'ł-k̓a,

The good people looked after the poor people,

7

á·yu-ił-du áɴ-łg̣ɛ́·n.

and then indeed they did not go hungry.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
