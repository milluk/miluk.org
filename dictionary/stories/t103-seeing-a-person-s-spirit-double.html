Seeing a person's spirit double

11 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

wí-yu-wu-c ɛ'yúctlɛ-dlú·g̣wa,

When I was living in Siuslau (country),

2

wí-mɛg̣ɛ́nt-du·-də́-k̓áʻ,

and the people were having a dance,

3

wí·-yu-kwí·-g̣ɛ́łəm wí·-qanu·dja·-ł-dlɛ́·g̣ɛq.

the next day we were sitting outside.

4

wí·-xʟlə́m·a tłə-máʻ-hum̯ɛ́·k̓ɛ,

Then the other woman went inside,

5

wí·-mí·t'cí-u-máʻ-da-dlú·g̣wa.

while I stayed (outside) there alone.

6

hɛi-má·tsi tlətc-hú·məs daʻ-cyə́ɣtciu,

Then indeed (another) woman went around (there),

7

q̓á·s dáɣəsəsa'adjá-kwi-wí·-du,

she went back of some huckleberry bushes,

8

hámaq̓'wə-í-ł·a.

I watched her as she went.

9

tsú-wədɛ́·djɛ,

Then I went into the house,

10

hɛi-má·tsi idzɛ́wətc kwi·-dlú·g̣wa'itc tłatc-hú·məs

and indeed there was the woman seated inside!

11

cyɛ'yúctłɛ-hú·məs.

She was a Siuslau woman.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
