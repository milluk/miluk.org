Talking too much

3 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

máⁱ'yukʷ ku-kwi·-q̓asə́ntc lə́t'sat kwə-nə'lɛ́i'lɛⁱ,

Even though your tongue wiggles with ease,

Linked dictionary entries (4)

ku that · kwi demonstrative pronoun · latsat tossing, wiggling · q̓asə́ntc with ease

2

wí-wá·g̣a-ditc x̣-kwí·-nə'wá·-ditc dzáitst

you make a very big thing (serious trouble) with it.

Linked dictionary entries (3)

dzaitst fix/ make · wa² large, big · kwi demonstrative pronoun

3

án·úk̓wa·niyada'í ɛ-kwi· wá·g̣a-ditc yu-kwi-gu·s-min-tłáx̣ats kwə-nə'lɛ́i'lɛⁱ

Do you not know that it might be a very big (serious) thing when your tongue flops around all the time?
