A shaman found a pain-power hidden in a dance house

15 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

x̣-wɛ́·n-ditc-du tłə-du·-x̣-íl·áx̣q̓ain kwi·-łú·dədaya,

That is why a shaman went and looked it over,

Linked dictionary entries (2)

íl·á́x̣q̓ain shanman (who causes/prevents death) · kwi demonstrative pronoun

he himself was first to examine where the people's dance house was.
