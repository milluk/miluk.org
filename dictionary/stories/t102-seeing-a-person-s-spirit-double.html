Seeing a person's spirit double

23 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

hit'cí g̣átq̓ai tsú-wəsílt̓.

I went outside one evening.

2

tləl-mit̓íya·dəs t'łúʻ-kwi·-tsq̓ɛ́.

The moon was hanging full.

3

hɛ́u-má·-tsi-u-kwí-k̯łá·wi má·tsi-tłgúsədjə kwi·-tɛ́iyə́x̣ɛu,

I saw him (Frank) go down to the ( river ) water to a (tłguus) canoe,

4

wɛ́n-g̣ɛ́ʻ-án-ditc.

and then there he vanished.

5

tsú-wudɛ́·djɛ.

I went into the house.

6

ní·k̓a̓

Mother

7

Frank-wuk̯łá·wi.

I saw Frank.

8

ník̓a

Mother

9

tsú-gu·s·ə́lt̓-də́-k̓a' tsú-iłwál·-wi.

The people all went outside and looked for him.

10

hɛi-má·tsi-áɴ-dá.

But he was not there.

11

h̓ə́ yɛ́·gwɛdɛ-x̯-dá-x̯-du.

Aha! it must have been his spirit double there (M. dł/maa̯k̓is).

12

má-g̣ahá·ya-g̯ɛ́ alicá·nu'ú-gum hɛi-má·tsi wə́-dzət'sí·-dik̓í-l·ga qa-'wá·ya.

The next day as I was playing I heard a baby bird.

13

tsú-wu-g̣ɛ́ʻ-ła.

I went to there.

14

hɛ́i-má·tsi ɛ́q-də́sɛʟ gwə́lgwlu·-du,

Indeed it was the head of a dead person rolling along,

15

i-du-wɛ́·n wá'nu gwa-dzə́t'si'i.

just like the way a (baby) bird does it.

16

tsú-u'wə́s·i.

I went back home.

17

ní·k̓a

Mother!

18

k̓áʻ-dəsɛʟ gwə́l·gwlai,

a person's head was rolling along,

19

wí-gwa-dzə́-t'si'í·kwi·-x̣áłtɛm.

doing it (chirping) like a bird.

20

m̓··· tsu·tsú·-k̓á·ʻ-dá-x̣,

Hm! A person must have been killed,

21

má·-da-x̣-hántł-k̓áʻ-tsú·tsu.

and another person is going to be killed.

22

ɛ́n·ɛ́-łəndítcix̯-hántł.

Maybe it will be one of our relatives.

23

tsú-tsi·-kwí·-ku-kúwi·

Now that is all of that.

← At death the heart went Seeing a person's spirit double →

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
