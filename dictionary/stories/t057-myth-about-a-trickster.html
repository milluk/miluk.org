Myth about a trickster

66 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

kwə́tc-t'smí·x̣wən há·saq̓

Myth about a trickster

2

yɛ́'ləs dlú·g̣wa,

Coyote lived (there),

3

hit'cí-di̯k̓íl·ga

he had one child.

4

wí-q̓áyau-tłə-dəhú·mis,

Now (when) his wife died,

5

x̣-mí·t'ci-kwi·-há·wau tłə-di̯k̓íl·ga,

he alone raised his child,

6

náyim l̯á·k̓is tłətc-tú·'mət'ł.

because (he) the old man was a widower.

7

wí-kwi-hɛ́·wi-tłə-di̯-k̓íl·ga

That child of his grew up.

8

dzəsdlaq̓áiʻ-nantł

Be (constantly) swimming!

9

hadái'məs də-na·-nántł łú·dəda·'ya

you should watch for wealth (t'l=x;i/nx;at).

10

wí·-inantł-kw̯í·-k̓íldwa wí-g̣ɛ́ʻ-nantł yúx̯da-nɛ́·wi.

And if you find it you should rub it there on yourself.

11

ma·-dú-antc k̯̓íl·dᵘwa.

But he never did find anything.

12

ú·· idjɛ́ʻ-k̓áʻ-nántł,

Oh you are going to be a poor and homely (ugly) person,

13

náim-na-án-wɛ́·n-wɛ́·tsəntq̓ɛ́m djí·-ni'ildá·mi.

because you haven't done anything yet what I have told you.

14

gu·c-djí-x̯áłtəm tłə-dí·lúł,

The young fellow did do everything (he could),

15

wí-ma·-dú-an-kú·wi

but he never (found) such a thing.

16

tsú-báłdicdja-dzəsdlá·q̓ai

Now he went swimming in the ocean.

17

tsú-há·p̓-didjá·na'niya tsí·m,

He lay then at the very edge of the water (at low tide),

18

gwa yu'tc-gwɛ-ɛ́q-tsí·m

he lay as if dead.

19

hɛi-má·tsi-dzə́nɛ́·-də-k̓aʻ,

And then people were coming along,

20

bɛ́··i-kwi-djín·i-tłə́-k̓áʻ.

the people were shouting as they came.

21

máʻ-da-tsí·m tłə-dí·lúł,

But the young man just lay there,

22

gwa'yút'c-gwɛ-ɛ́q-tsí·m

he lay as if dead.

23

tsú-iłt'cíl·iya'ił-tłí-ił-tłə-hɛ́məlt̓

Now they made themselves a fire.

24

tsú-hit'cí-k̓áʻ-tɛ́·ix̣ɛu

Then one of the people came down towards the water.

25

gwa'yút'c-gwɛ-ɛ́q-tsí-m.

He lay as if he were dead.

26

cyə́··tcitc,

He circled around him,

27

t'łx̣áini-tłə-x̯-k̓áʻ-g̣ɛ́ʻ-dji,

the person who came there examined him,

28

hɛi-má·tsi-yag̣áda'itc

and then he ran back.

29

sədlə́ntc du·-hamáq̓ tłɛ-x̣-dí·luł.

The young man looked at him on the sly.

30

tsu-iłx̯ín·iya-iłdəkwɛ́ʻ hɛ́məlt̓ədjə

Now they put (hot rocks) into their bucket by the fire.

31

tsú-du-hɛlt̓-má·-k̓aʻ-dzənɛ

And then some other person came to him.

32

tsú-hantł-du-dimt̓í·mu

They were going to pack him.

33

tsú-du-gwə́l·i'ya du·-dux̣wálx̣wal,

So now he rolled his eyes (to frighten away this man),

34

tsú-du·-nɛ́qhɛ tłə-k̓áʻ kwⁱ·-yantł-du·-t̓á·mi

and the person who had come to pack him fled.

35

tsú-hɛthɛ́·dɛdə-tɛ́·ix̣ɛu,

Now then the wealthy head man came down to the water,

36

má·tsi-gwáʻ-qʷdlí·s tłə-dəx̣-ás·da,

with his decorations he was like foam,

37

gú·s-kwi-hadái'məs tłə-dətɛ·'tc

his clothes were completely money (large dentalia).

38

tsú-kwí·-djí,

Then he got there,

39

tsú-g̣·álam-tłɛ-x̣-dí·lúł

and the young man seized him.

40

áɴ-dji-dzɛ́m-t̓u ya-g̣álam tłɛ-x̣-dí·lúł

He could not pull himself loose when the dilu caught him.

41

tsú-máʻ-g̣ɛ́ʻ-na'qt.

And he just held on tightly.

42

tsú-ił-kwi--x̣-gú-s-g̣ɛ́ʻ-łá,

Now they all went to there,

43

kłá·wi'ił tłə-kwi-náqt tlɛ-x̣-dí·lúł tłi-ildɛhɛthɛ́·dɛ.

they saw the young man holding their head man.

44

tsú-a·uct̓ kwí-iłnɛhɛ́

Now come on!

45

məlt̓idjə

come to our fire with us!

46

g̣ɛ́ʻ-ła'ai.

He went there.

47

tsú-ił-má·tsi-páukʷts tłə-dí·lúł,

Then they simply stole the young man,

48

tsú-iłwəsá·ya,

they took him towards their home,

49

hɛi-má·tsi x̣-maqt'łí-kwi-xwáiłu,

being just crows they flew aloft,

50

tsú-iłt̓á·mi tłə-dí·luł.

and they carried the young man with them.

51

tsú-iłyú·yu,

Now they halted,

52

wúł-init̓ɛ-tɛ́·ix̣ɛu dɛ-ɛ́stis.

some of them went down to the water to dig mussels.

53

tsú-t'cdjáʻ tłə-də'wá·l'wail tlɛ-x̣-dí·lúł,

Now the young man pulled out his knife

54

ní·m-kwə-nahadái'məs.

Give me your money (large dentalia).

55

tsúwa'mí·náx̣ inantł-án-hadái'məs ní·m.

I will kill you if you do not give me money.

56

á·yu-hadái'məs-níya

Sure enough they gave him money.

57

an-u-g̣á·ł-wudú·ha'ya kwə-nəhadái'məs.

I want a quantity of your money.

58

inantł-án-łu·-ní·m wi-tsúwa'mi·nántł.

If you do not give it I will surely kill you.

59

á·yu-łu-níya.

So indeed they gave it to him.

60

tsú-í·g̣ɛi tłə-dí·lúł,

Then the young man went away,

61

wɛ́n-wə́s·i.

and he went back towards home.

62

hadái'-məs-dət̓ím kwi·-wɛ́·st.

He had a pack (loaded) with money when he returned.

63

ɛ́··. nə̯x̣-k̓íl·ga

ee/ee my child!

64

tsú-nət'łx̯ín·x̯at

Now (you have) your encounter-wealth!

65

tsúʻ-tsi·-kúwi tłə-t'smí·x̣wən-dəłag̣áwiyát̓a·s,

And that is that trickster's tale,

66

tłə-t'smí·x̣wən bá·saq̓

that trickster myth.
