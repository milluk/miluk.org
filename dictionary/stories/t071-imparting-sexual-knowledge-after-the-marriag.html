Imparting sexual knowledge after the marriage payment

34 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

i-du-tłu·tłúyu tłə-gwɛ́is,

When they had purchased the girl,

2

wí-i-du-hɛhɛ́mildju'wí·dɛ,

and when she was to be taken to her husband,

3

wí-x̣-ɛn·ɛ-dú·-du-wɛ́n-íldwa.

this is what her mother would say to her.

4

wí-sg̣ú·ya dú-tlə-di̯k̓ílga,

She would tell her child,

5

djí·-nantł-dúhidu·n tłə-nɛx̣-dɛ· mił,

Whatever your husband will want of you,

6

ma·-nántl-du-x̣-wɛ́·n.

that is the way you are to do it.

7

i-nantł-g̣wá·niyu·nɛ dú·ha'ya x̯ín·í'yim,

When he will want to get on top of you,

8

wí-i-nanta g̣ɛʻ-djí n̥dúhidu·n,

and he wants something (intercourse) of you,

9

wí-ma-nántł-tłq̓ɛ́ⁿ.

you accede to that.

10

gú·s-k̓áʻ-x̣-wɛ·n i-du-k̓áʻ-hú·msiyu.

All people do it like that when they get wives.

11

wí-gú·s-íł-x̣-wɛ·n,

They are all like that,

12

náim-du-gú·s wíʻ-wa·ndi·da.

because everybody does it.

13

hɛ́lɛ'yu-tłantł kwə-du-k̓á-q̓álaⁿ.

It will hurt a person the first time only.

14

dá-qłəmniyu an-dú-da·s-x̣ɛ́nwəs.

After that there is no hurt (pain) any more.

15

x̣-wɛ́n-du-íldwa tłə-dəgwá·ya.

That is the way she spoke to her daughter.

16

áyú-du-máʻ-wɛ́·n i-hɛhɛ́mildju.

Indeed that's how it's when she's delivered to her husband.

17

wí-djí-tłə-dɛx̣-ɛ́nɛ íldwa,

just as her mother had told her,

18

máʻ-x̣-wɛ́·n.

so she did.

19

yantł-djíndji·nu kwə-nəhú·mis,

When your wife will be fetched,

20

an·ántł-djilt'sá·ya.

you must not be bashful before her.

21

wɛ́·n-ditc-du.

That is how it must be.

22

i-nantł-tsú·wí'ya wí-x̣índidzá·ma-nantł,

When you go to bed with her you will climb on top of her,

23

wí-hɛidzitc-nantł dú·ha'ya.

and then you will attempt copulation.

24

x̣-wɛ́n-du-íldwa tłɛ-x̣-dɛ́·mil tłə-dədlə́l·wɛ.

That is the way a man spoke to his son.

25

wɛ́n-du-bahána'naya.

That is how he advised him.

26

mái-nantł-gwɛ-x̣-djíʻ-naqáutc i-nantl-hatsháya,

When you feel something queer when you copulate with her,

27

wí-gwantł-x̣-wɛ́n nəqáutc.

that is how it feels as if queer.

28

wí-ma-nántł an-í·g̣ɛⁱ,

Then you must not get away (withdraw),

29

an·ántł pín·ti'mí·dɛ,

you will want to (as if) urinate,

30

tsí-nantł-tłgá·t's,

and then you will ejaculate,

31

an·ántł-ɛ·wi.

you will not quit.

32

tsú-gwi-gúmt̓u,

When finished with the queer feeling,

33

tsúʻ-nɛ'ɛ́·wi.

then you may quit.

34

tsí-du-lúwidɛ ku-hú·mis-g̣ag̣álmu.

That is what you have wanted to get a wife for.
