Stories

There were many people at that place

81 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

g̣á·l k̓áʻ mít'łda'yá·s

There were many people at that place

2

tsú-x̣-wɛ́·n sqwɛ·yɛ́nu də-k̓áʻ,

Now the people were spreading this news,

3

gu·s-wí·-tsantł g̣ɛ́-hit'cú·nu'wɛyɛ

they said that everybody was going to gather there.

4

tsú-wɛn-íl·dᵘwa tłə-dihí-mɛ tłɛ-x̣-t'smí·x̣wən,

Then this is what the trickster told his children,

5

ła'ís-hantł,

You must go,

6

kán·i'is-hántł

you must attempt it.

7

g̣á·ł-hadái'mis dəg̣wúla·qʷ.

Its value (cost) is quantities of money (large dentalia).

8

łú·-tsántł-dzag̣ág̣aʻ x̣-g̣aháisitc.

They say it will be hanging down from the (sky) above.

9

misá-is-hantł-kwi·-kán·i

You must both try.

10

wá··g̣aʻ-ditc.

(It is) a very big (costly) thing.

11

wí-is-hantł-g̣álám wí-nɛqɛ́'is-hantł

If you should get it you will flee,

12

wí-wucdjɛ́u'is k̯̓ínau,

whichever one of you tires,

13

wí-hɛlt̓-hántł du-x̣-má'ais is-du·-hwə́ldiya.

then the other one of you will dash on with it.

14

qʻdíl-q̓ham-tsantł kwə-łɛ́·ł kwá·ntł kwí· x̣ug̣ú-dzag̣ág̣a.

They say they will try to shoot down that medicine which will be hanging from above

15

ə́ⁿ·· kán·íʻ-nantł.

Yes. We will attempt it.

16

á-yu-ła'áyam tłə-tcán·əɣa

Indeed the boys went.

17

k̯̓ílɛ́·-s-hantł łú·dədaya.

We will take great care.

18

xmí·mtc-is-hántł ditc-x̣áłi.

We will have to do it rapidly.

19

á·yu-maʻ-wɛ́·n tłə-tcán·əɣa.

Indeed the boys did so.

20

aɴ-ítcg̣ɛ́qł,

They did not sleep,

21

q̓ɛłí·mis-itc dlɛ́·q-sim,

they arose early in the morning, and then they went.

22

tsú-itcłá·á·yu má·n-g̣ɛʻ hit'cúwɛᵘ-də̯-k̓a'.

Sure enough people were already gathered there.

23

tsú-his-tłə-tú·'mít'ł łá

Then the old man (the trickster himself) went too.

24

lú·dədaya-wántł kwə-'nihí·mɛ

I will watch and take care of my children.

25

yúwút's-íł djiʻ-ayá·dja·t̓a.

It is possible they might do something (harmful) to them.

26

gwa'yú·t'c-wantł łə́n·wi-tú·'mit'ł-ditc

I will pretend that I am an extremely old man.

27

wí-áɴ-hantł wɛ́·n-iłdi'lúwɛ ku-wu-dáʻ.

Then they will not suppose I am there.

28

áyu-mɛ-x̣-wɛ́·n· his-hídji łá.

So he did. He went too.

29

áɴ-k̓wá·níyada tłə-dɛx̣-hí·mɛ tłə-his-hídji-dá ʻ

His children did not know that he was there.

30

tsú-áyu-g̣aqdílyim-də-k̓áʻ

Now indeed the people started to shoot at it.

31

tsú-tłu-tcánəɣa

Now the boys (said),

32

áɴ-s-hantł má·tsi-djí·ʻ,

We will not just do it badly (by shooting at the string),

33

yú-wəntł du-qdál,

when I shoot (I will shoot rather at the sky itself),

34

wí-yi-lə́m·ya isgá-hantł-lə́m·ya.

if it strikes in we will know that it is stuck in.

35

wí-his-nantł-nɛ́u gɛ́ʻ-nantł-sk̓wát.

And then you will shoot there also.

36

á·yu-maʻ-wɛ́·n tlə-dí·lúł.

To be sure, that is what the young men did.

37

áyu-itc k̯łá·wi tłi-itcdu'wú-tam,

Now they saw their arrows (hit the sky),

38

á·yu-itc-máʻ-x̣-wɛ́·n qʻdál.

and indeed they kept on shooting like that.

39

tsú-gwá·-hwə́łɛ·t dəgwá·'níya,

Now it appeared as if it was (not too) high for a jump up,

40

tsú-hit'ci-du-hwə́l·di g̣ɛ́ʻ tłə-dí·lúł.

and so one of the young men leaped up to it there.

41

hís-nántł-nɛ́u.

Now you do it too.

42

áyu-itc-dáʻ-hɛ́l·ɛ́q tłi-itcdu'wúsbaya'atc

And sure enough they ascended there on their arrows.

43

áɴ-x̣-wi-kwi djíʻ túwittsa·t̓a,

The others were unable to make it fall down,

44

hɛ́łt̓-ha-g̣ú.

it was too high up.

45

mái'yukʷ- kwi·-k̯inká·n wí-an-iłg̣álmí·t̓a

Though many of them tried they could not get it.

46

tsú-itc-g̣ɛ́ʻ-djiʻ tłə-tcán·əɣa

Now then the boys had gotten up there.

47

kwí'-ya-k̓xí tłə-də-ləx̣ál·ax̣

Hit the string now!

48

á·yu tłə-dí·lúł,

Indeed the young man did,

49

ha̯-k̓ílɛ kwi·-k̓xá tsú-hál·mi tłɛ-x̯hít'ci tłɛ-x̣-tcán·əɣa

he hit it so well ( well enough ) that the other boy seized it,

50

á·yu g̣álám

and then sure enough he had it.

51

tsú-itcpq̓áᵘ.

Now they descended.

52

dí· g̣álám

Take this!

53

kwə-'ni-ts'áidja g̣ɛ́ʻ-nantł-tsx̣á·t's

step on my back!

54

á·yu-ma-x̣-wɛ́·n,

He did so,

55

tsú-itcg̣adlɛ́·'níyɛ.

and now they had gotten down.

56

tsú-tlə-dəhɛt'łɛ́·didjɛ tsx̣á·t's,

Then he stepped on his older brother,

57

tsú-hwə́l·di,

and then he leaped,

58

á·yu nəkwí'yu tłə-łɛ́·ł.

and so he dashed away with the medicine.

59

má·n-u'ú-idu

Now they pursued him.

60

tsú-hwə́l·di-tłə-tú'mit'ł,

Then the old man ran,

61

gwa'yút'c tú·ya,

he pretended that he fell,

62

hit'c-áɴ tú·ya tłə-tú·'mit'ł,

the old man did not really fall,

63

tsi-tá·ts-dət̓ɛ

he just threw himself down.

64

á·yu-gu·s-k̓á g̣ɛʻ-tú·ya

Indeed all the people tumbled there (over him).

65

tsú-his-hidji yag̣áda tłə-dí·-lúł.

Now the other young man was also running.

66

x-ɛ́n·ɛ-wantł-łá·i wí-łhɛtə́m-nantł.

I myself will go along with it while you rest.

67

wí-tsú-wantł ga·síya g·idí·wiya,

And when they have pretty nearly caught up with me,

68

tsú-nantł-du hɛ́łt̓-nɛ́u.

then you can (run with it).

69

á·yu-itc-ma-x̣-wɛ́·n.

Sure enough that is the way they did it,

70

wɛ́n-tłə-tú'mit'ł hís-du-hi'djiʻ hwuthwí·d.

And the old man ran along too.

71

tsú-du-ga·síya g·idí·wiya tłə-dəhí·mɛ,

Now they had almost caught up to his children,

72

tsú-du·-tú·ya k̓áʻ-dəhɛ́l·ɛ'yu,

and then he fell down ahead of the people,

73

á·yu-du-g̣ɛ́ʻ-q̓dláts tlə-k̓áʻ

and indeed the people tumbled there (over that man).

74

á·yu-du-x̣-kwi-há·ya'ats tłə-tcán·-əɣa.

And so that made the boys get far ahead.

75

wɛ́n-iłłáya-tłə-łɛ́·ł,

In that manner they obtained the medicine,

76

á·yu-iłhɛdíyɛ

and indeed they-became wealthy (by selling it).

77

áɴ-x̣-wi-k̓wá·ni'yada tlə-kwi·-'ya tłə-łɛ́·ł,

No one knew just who had stolen the medicine,

78

hít'c tłɛ-x̣-t'smí·x̣wən dihí·mɛ tłə-kwí·-g̣álám tłə-łɛ́·ł.

but it really was the trickster's children who had gotten the medicine.

79

x̣-wɛ́n-itc-kwi·-g̣álám,

That was the way they got it,

80

kwi'yú-iłhɛdíyɛ tłɛ-łɛ́·ł

and they became rich from the medicine.

81

tsú-tsi·-wɛ́·s

Now so much of that.
