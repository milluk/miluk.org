The kind of wife to select

22 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

i-nantł-húmas-dú·ha'ya lú·dədá·ya-nantł-nit̓ɛ.

When you desire a wife watch out carefully for yourself.

2

yu-hú·mis án-k̓a-x̯ílat̓,

If a woman does not look at people,

3

ámi-xwíyɛs,

never smiles,

4

gu·s-mín g̣ɛdlɛ́·n k̓á·yim,

looks down all the time,

5

áɴ-dú·ha'ya k̓áʻ-hámaq̓,

appears as if she does not want to see people,

6

wí· áɴ-wɛ·n-hú·misí·dɛ.

she will be no good as a wife.

7

dji· ́xa-kwi-cít'cdi,

She is like a channel,

8

hɛ́ni̯·k̓is-gwá-ł·u.

that is what she is like.

9

g̣á·ł-din ɛ́·djis t'smí·-x̣wn,

(She is) tricky in all sorts of things,

10

áɴ-wɛ·n.

(she is) no good.

11

wí-i̯-k̓ɛ́·lɛ-hí·mɛ k̯̓ɛ́·lɛ-hú·misí·dɛ,

But she who is a good child and going to become a good wife,

12

wí·-áɴ-djilt'sdu,

she is not bashful,

13

nɛg̣ɛ́nɛ́·-djɛ,

she jokes,

14

isgáʻ-tli i-n̯k̓x̯idíya'yɛis.

she speaks forth when you address her.

15

yisgáʻ-g̣ɛg̣ɛ́nɛdjɛ́·ni'wɛ,

She speaks and jokes,

16

wɛn-hɛ́ldjɛ·-nɛhɛ́mɛ·-q̓ɛin,

she looks you right in the face,

17

án-k̯mɛ́·nɛn.

not with downcast head.

18

kwi·-hántl-kwə-nəg̣álam.

That is the kind you should get.

19

k̯̓ɛ́·łɛ-hú·mis-hantł.

She will be a good wife.

20

an-hántł-t'smí·x̣wn mái-yu-kwi-gá·ł-dəg̣ɛ́nɛ́tc.

She will not be tricky even though she may joke a lot.

21

w̯í-k̓ɛ́·lɛ-hú·mis-hantł.

She will be a good wife.

22

wi-i-djílt'sdu-wí-aɴ-wɛ·n t'smí·xwn.

A bashful one is no good and (is) tricky.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
