What shamans could see

6 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

i-du-ditc-íłdə́g̣wəns,

Whatever their dream (power),

2

wí-i-tím·íłt̓u,

when it becomes strong,

3

wí-mái-ditc' ma-íł-kwi·-hámá·q̓

no matter what it is they indeed see through it.

4

ái-idzɛ́wtc-dlú·g̣wa má-qánu·dja-ditc k̯lá·wi.

Even though he may be staying inside he (can) see outside.

5

wí-mái̯-k̓ibíndjɛ má-gala'yú·dja k̯łá·wi tłɛ́-k̓xáⁱ tłu-wá·-íl·áx̣q̓ain.

A powerful shaman (strong ) even sees a pain-power inside a person.

6

wɛ́n-ditc-du tłə-kwí·-k̓wa·-níyada tlə-ditc x̣ɛ́nwə'ɛs tłɛ-x̣-kwí·-x̣ɛ́nwəs-wá·wa

That is why he knows whatever sickness (it is) that has made him (a patient) ill.

← Shamans cured tuberculosis A shaman found a pain-power hidden in a dance house →

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
