Anger at seeing a rainbow

9 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

ú·yu uk̯łá·wi.

I see a rainbow.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

ú·yu rainbow

2

h̓ɛ́· idjáu-da-x̯ k̓á tłhwíya.

Humph! someone must be giving birth.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

da Modal auxiliary must · ídjɛ² where/ there · k̯̓a' people, person · tlhwi gave birth to

3

náu da kwi ú·yu tlɣí·lɛl.

That is why the rainbow is in the form of a bow.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

da Modal auxiliary must · kwi demonstrative pronoun · náu that is, for that .../ because · ú·yu rainbow
