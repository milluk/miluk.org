A girl found a husband

37 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tłə-gwɛ́is gu·s-mín-du-yuxwúmɛ qłím.

A girl traveled about at night all the time.

2

gu·s-mín-du-wɛ́n-íldwa tłə-dəx̣-má·ni'yá·s.

Her parents always told her thus.

3

an·ántł-k̓·ínau.

Do not become tired.

4

yuxʷumɛ́-nantł.

Travel here and there.

5

wí·-kwi·-x̣-g̣aháis-nantł k̓wí·ni·du·n.

Then a day (a dream-power) will take pity on you.

6

áyú-du-mɛ́-x̣-wɛ́n tłə-gwɛ́is.

Indeed the girl did so.

7

há·p̓ədjáʻ-dji,

She came to water,

8

wí·-dáʻ-du-dzəsdlá·q̓aⁱ.

and there she swam in it.

9

án-i·da-ditc-álqsa.

She feared nothing then.

10

tłə-du-kwí·-x̣-wɛ́n-wá·tsa tłə-dik̓·ílga tłɛ-x̣-k̓á.

That is why people did that to their children.

11

hɛⁱ-dú-á·yu-ítc yuwút's-ditc-k̓·íldwa wɛ́n-hít'ci tłə-gwɛ́is.

Sure enough maybe some one girl sometimes 'd find something in that manner.

12

dzəs-dlá·q̓aⁱ.

She would be swimming.

13

hɛⁱ-má·tsi tłə-dəwɛ́·t'łədjə k̓á dlú·g̣wa.

Now there was a person seated right on her garment.

14

ní·m-kwə-'nəwɛ́·t'ł

Give me my dress

15

áɴ-láix̣t tłə-dɛ́·mił.

The man did not budge.

16

g̣ɛ́-dlú·g̣wa tłə-dəwɛ́·t'łə́djɛ.

He kept seated there on her dress.

17

ní·m-kwə-'nəwɛ́·t'ł'

Give me my dress

18

tsú-á·yu níya-tłə-dəwɛ́·t'ł,

Then to be sure he did give her the garment,

19

tsú-wɛn-íldwa,

so she spoke to him thus,

20

'ɛ́ʻdji̓

Come on

21

wəs·í-wantł.

I am going back home.

22

á·yu-itc wusəsíyam.

So indeed they went home.

23

qánu·-sdúq tłə-dɛ́·mił,

The man stood outside,

24

wí-dɛ́·djɛ tłə-gwɛ́is.

and the girl went in.

25

ní·k̓a

Mother

26

dɛ́·mil-uk̓·íldwa,

I have found a man,

27

wí-kwí-u'wá·sda.

and I have brought him home.

28

tsú-sg̣ú·ya tłə-də-dɛ́·mił tłɛ-x̣-hú·mis.

The woman then informed her husband.

29

łá·dza.

Go get him.

30

dɛ́·dja-hántł.

Bring him inside.

31

tsú-á·yu.

Then indeed (she did).

32

án-mit'ssiya dá· dəɣála tłɛ-x̣-dɛ́·mił.

The man did not know their language.

33

hə́·· nəx̣-k̓·ílga̓

Ah! my daughter

34

tsú-nət'łxánx̣t.

Now he is your good-luck-power.

35

x̣-wɛ́·n-du tłə-'nə-wɛ́n-í·ldá·mi.

That is why I told you so.

36

x̣-wɛ́n-íldwa tłə-dəgwá·ya.

That is what he said to his daughter.

37

kwí·-tłə-dət'łx̣ínx̣.

He was her good-luck-power.
