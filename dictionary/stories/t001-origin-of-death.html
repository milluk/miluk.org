Origin of death

55 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

sła'á·djinu-itc.

They were two friends.

2

kwí-íł łɛqlú·t̓tsəm.

They lived there.

3

wí-misá-itc nəhúmɛ·k,

Both of them had wives.

4

ɛ-hɛ· misá-itc ɛ·t'cíłi-itcditímłi-hí·mɛ.

Both had little boy babies.

5

mín·t'ci g̣ɛ́łəm tsu-kwí·-x̣ɛnx̣ɛnu tłi̯-k̓ílga.

One morning that child (of one of them) became ill.

6

án-hɛ·niyɛ x̣ɛ́nwəs tłi̯-k̓ílga.

His child was not ill long.

7

wí·-kwi·-q̓áyaᵘ tłi̯-k̓ílga.

Then the child just died.

8

x̣an'wíya-lúwɛ tłi̯-k̓ílga,

He was so sad about his child,

9

yu-kwí·-q̓áyaᵘ.

when it died.

10

tsú·-ił'ág̣a·'ná·ya.

Then they buried it.

11

ma'áis áɴ-dlu'wiyam.

The next day he did not eat.

12

ka'ná·ya tłə-di̯k̓íłga.

He continually looked for his child.

13

dzáwa-g̣ahá·ya kwí·-yuxʷúmadá·ya tłə-dəs'łá'a.

On the fourth morning he went to his friend.

14

djinhɛhɛ́·nuni'í

What do you think?

15

dá·i-'nəʻs'lá

my friend

16

djí·-x̯-na'lúwɛ g̣ɛnt'cínsɛ·n g̣ɛ́łəm an-áx̣-tłə-wɛ·st'í tłə-'ni̯k̓ílga

do you think my child will ( come back ) return in five days?

17

wɛ́·n-íl·at.

That is what he said.

18

an-g̯ɛ́·-s'lá

Oh no friend

19

má·tsi dítc-łdjɛʻ

just go and eat

20

w̯í·k̓ilɛ́·t-nantł-lúwɛ.

then you will feel better.

21

wɛ́·n íldᵘwa.

That is what he said to him.

22

tsú-má·tsi áɴ-dji di'łúwɛ.

He did not know what to think now.

23

má·tsi x-wɛn djinhɛhɛ́·nu,

So he just figured,

24

dlə́lyudá-mi-nantłi.

I,ll surely get even with you.

25

áyú án-ha-hɛ́·niyɛ áyú x̣ɛ́n-xɛnu tłə-di̯k̓ílga.

Indeed it was not long before his child became ill (too).

26

án-hɛ·niyɛ-x̣ɛ́nwəs·.

It was not sick long.

27

mán-ma·tsi q̓áyaᵘ.

(Now) already it was dead.

28

hɛi-gwa-áɴ áɴ-wɛn-di'lúwɛ,

He was very sick at heart,

29

yu-kwi· q̓áyaᵘ tłə-di̯k̓ílga.

when his child died.

30

á·yu x̯-kwí'ya tłə-kwí·-wɛn íl·-at,

Indeed it was as if he had spoken that way,

31

tłə-kwí·-dúha'ya wɛ́·st tłə-di̯k̓ílga.

when he had wanted his child to return.

32

tsú-áyú g̣ɛ́ʻ-ła.

Then indeed he (too) went there.

33

dái s'lá

34

35

36

wɛ́·n-íłdᵘwa.

That is what he said to him.

37

án-g̯ɛ́· s'lá

Oh no indeed my friend

38

má·tsi-nantł dlu'wíyam

You just go and eat

39

w̯í·-k̓ilɛ·t-nantł-lúwɛ.

and then your heart will feel better.

40

wɛ́·n-íldᵘwa.

That is what he said.

41

g̣áida-hantł wá·sdidiyam tłi·s-nihí·mɛ,

42

wɛ́·n-kwi·-má·tsi áɴ-du·ha'ya x̣-wɛ́·n.

but you did not want that.

43

hɛi-hántłi máʻ-aɴ-wɛ́·s-didi,

44

45

46

k̯̓ílɛ́·-x̯ kwi-ya tłə-nə-wɛ́·n-íl·dai.

47

wɛ́·n djinhɛhɛ́·nu.

That is what he thought.

48

m̯á·-k̓íłɛ tłə-kwí·-wɛ́·n-íl·dᵘwa,

It would have been better had he spoken to him,

49

g̣ɛnt'cínsi'yɛ-n g̣ɛ́łəm t̓ə́ma-ax̣tłə-du-wɛ́·sdədi,

After five mornings they may return,

50

ya-áx̣tłə-wɛ́·n-tłi.

had he said that to him.

51

k̯̓ɛ́·lɛ-ax̣tłəʟ,

It would have been good,

52

ya-ax̣tłə g̣ɛnt'cínsi g̣aháis wɛ́·sdidi,

could he have returned in five days,

53

tłə-k̓á-q̓áyaᵘ.

a person who had died.

54

tsúmáʻ-da-djɛ́·nɛn.

Now it finished there.

55

wɛ́·n iłlagáwiyat̓a·ná'ya.

That is the way the myth was told by them.
