The woman who dreamt, but did not do what her dream told her

41 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tłə-hú·mis gwa'at̓ə́sdə,

The woman who dreamt,

2

áɴ-ma-x̣-wɛ́·n djí-íldwa tłə-dɛx̣-g̣wə́ns

but who did not do what her dream told her

3

hú·mis kwí·-báldi·mis dəg̣wə́ns,

A woman had an ocean dream (power),

4

wɛ́n-his-tq̓áyau.

and also dentalium (dream power).

5

wí·-kwi·-wá·-íl·-áx̯q̓ain.

She had already become a great shaman.

6

wi·-wɛ́n-íl·dwa tłə-dɛx̣-g̣wə́ns.

Now this is what her (new) dream told her.

7

in·antł-g̣ɛ́ʻ-ła,

When you go to there (to Mussel Reef village on the coast),

8

wí·-dlukʷdá·t-nantł kwə-nɛhɛ́l.

you must cover your face.

9

a·nántł-x̣í·la,

You must not look (at the sea),

10

mái-nantł-kwi·-qa'wá·ya sáihⁿhwíyam kwətc-dzə́nɛ.

even if you hear the jingling as they come ashore.

11

an·ántł-xí·la.

You must not look.

12

i-nantł-xí·la,

If you do look,

13

wí-an-hantł-łu hithí·t̓.

none of them will drift ashore.

14

wí-i-na-áɴ-x̯í·la,

But if you do not look,

15

wi·-g̣á·ł-hantł kwa-hadái'mis g̣á-hantł-dukwi-dán·danaⁱ.

quantities of dentalia ( dentalia ) (quantities) will come ashore.

16

wi-x̣-k̓aʻ hantł-du kwi·-g̯its̯k̓ím·ats.

The people will pick them up.

17

wí-x̣-nɛ́u wí-an·ántł-du t'swá·lal

But you yourself must not bother with them.

18

i-nantł hit'cíg̣álám,

If you pick up just one,

19

wí-wəsitsə́m-hantł.

they (all) will return to the water (vanish).

20

má·tsi-hantł-pxá·wəl g̣ɛʻ-dán·danaⁱ,

Only periwinkles will come in there (then, and in future),

21

i-nantł-tłɛ́ʻ-x̯í·la.

even if you look quickly.

22

áyu-g̣ɛ-łá·yaⁱ tłə-yɛ́l·ɛ́·s,

Now they went to the shaman's dance there,

23

áyu-g̣ɛ́ʻ-djíʻ-də́-k̓aʻ.

now the people arrived there.

24

á·yu-g̣ɛ́ ʻməg̣ániya tłə-íl·áx̣q̓ain.

At length the shaman (woman) began to sing and dance there.

25

hadái'mis-hántł-hɛ́gwən

Money will come in from the water

26

báldi·mis hántł-kwi-hagwə́nya

The ocean will bring it ashore

27

á·yu gwa-t'sí·x̯ix̯iyam yu-kwi-dzə́nɛ tła-hadái'mis.

It was like jingling when the money came in then, indeed.

28

gú·s-k̓aʻ-kwí· ha-dú·-ha'ya hámaq̓,

Everybody wanted to watch,

29

ya-hántł-kwí-hɛ́gwən tła-hadái'mis.

when the money would come ashore.

30

tsú-sə́lt̓-də́-k̓aʻ,

The people went outside (in great excitement),

31

ha·-íłdú·-ha'ya kwi-íłk·łá·wi.

they wanted so very much to see it.

32

tsú-his-tłə-íl·áx̣q̓ain x̯í·la,

Now the shaman looked (at the water) too,

33

yu-kwí·-gwa-tccáixuxwiyam tłə-hadái'mis.

when the money jingled.

34

á·yu-mɛ-x̣-wɛ́·n.

But this is how it was indeed.

35

má·tsi pxá·wəl-hí·q̓ɛⁿ,

Only periwinkles came on the beach,

36

yu-kwí·-x̯í·la tłə-íl·áx̣q̓ain.

when the shaman looked.

37

wí-yu-kwi-dá-qłə́mniyu wí-yuwút's nict'c g̯i̯k̓íl·idu tła-hadái'mis dáʻ.

After that they sometimes found just a very few valuables (dentalia) there.

38

x̣-wɛ́n wəs·ítsim tłə-dəg̣wə́ns.

That is how her dream (power) left her.

39

x̣-wɛ́n tłə-dəg̣wə́ns á·yu kwi-áɴ-ditc,

Indeed that is the way her dream was as nothing,

40

náim-x̯í·la·

because she looked.

41

tsú-tsi-wɛ́·s.

Now that is all.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
