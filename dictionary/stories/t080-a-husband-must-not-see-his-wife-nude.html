A husband must not see his wife nude

41 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tłə-k̓áʻ,

A person,

2

tłə-t'łí·n-k̓aʻ,

a straight person (i.e., a well-to-do woman),

3

án-x̣-wi·-kwi·-hámáq̓ tłə-dədzɛ́·t'łis,

no one may ever look at her skin (i.e., genitals),

4

mái-humɛ́·-k̓ɛ án-ił-kwi·-hamaq̓.

even other women may not look at it.

5

wí-in-dɛ́·míłɛ wí-his-gúma tłə-dɛx̣-dɛ́·mił án-hámaq̓ tłə-dəmáʻ-ditc.

Even her very husband may not look at her something (genitals).

6

x̣-wɛ́·n tłə́-k̓áʻ,

That is why a (well-to-do) person,

7

yɛ-x̣-dɛ́·miłdə́-ta·ts wí-k̯łá·wi tłə-dədítc,

if her own husband threw her down and saw her thing

8

wí-łín·wi-hú·dədá·wa·s.

that was a strong (great) insult.

9

wí-má·ni'ya·sdjadə-wə́s·i.

She would go back home to her parents.

10

wí-yi-mínt'cíyu,

She would be asked,

11

djí-yɛ́t̓gɛ-ɛnə'wɛ́·st

Why have you come back home?

12

hú·didi·n'wi

He insulted me.

13

wɛ́·n-ditc ɛ'u'wɛ́·st.

That is why I have come back home.

14

ámi-u-wɛ́t'ł titsú·n,

He threw me down without my garments (covering me),

15

wɛ́n-kwi-dú·ha'ya-hámaq̓ tłə-'nədítc.

and then he wanted to see my thing.

16

x̣-wɛ́n-ditc-du-u'wɛ́·st.

That is why I have returned home.

17

k̯̓ɛ́·lɛ'-yəkʷ

Oh very well indeed!

18

á·yu-du k̓áʻ-du-g̣ɛ́ʻ-wəłx̯iyu.

They sent a person to there.

19

á·yu-du-g̣ɛ́ʻ-łá tłə-n̯ík̓taháiwəs.

The intermediary went to there.

20

tsú-du·-wɛ́·st.

He came back.

21

h̓ú an-tsántł-tsłədzíya

Humph! he says he will not pay for it!

22

k̯̓ɛ́·lɛ'yəkʷ

Very well then!

23

wɛ́nantł-íldwa ya-an-dú·ha'ya tsłá·ts,

You will tell him that if he does not want to pay for it,

24

wulmɛ́uł-hantł,

we will fight it out,

25

náim-hú·dədawa·s dá-x̣-du.

because he did it as an insult.

26

áyu-mɛ́-x̯-wɛ·n.

That is how it was indeed.

27

yúwút's íł-łu·-daqdlái wułmɛ́ᵘ.

They sometimes fought over that sort of thing.

28

wɛ́·n-ditc tłə-kwi-wá·g̣a-ditc i-dɛ́·mił x̣-wɛ́n wá·tsan-dəhú·mis,

That's why it was big thing if a husband did that to his wife,

29

kwi·-dú·ha'ya k̯łá·wi-dəditc.

and wanted to see her thing.

30

hú·dədáwa·s-də.

It was an insult.

31

náu-du-lín·wi sk̓át-wá·wa tłɛ́-x̣-k̓áʻ.

That is why the people made a strong fine payment for that.

32

x̣-wɛ́·n-ditc tłi-ił-án-hum̯ɛ́·k̓ɛ'iłdə wɛ́·n-wa·tsa,

That is why they did not do that to their wives,

33

náim-ił'ɛ́lqs.

because they were afraid.

34

an-dú-ɛłɛ́·də-g̣wə́sg̣wái.

She never told it to her father.

35

wi-yi-at̓á·d(th)ə-dá',

If her father's sister was there,

36

wi-kwí·-sg̣ú·ya.

she would tell her.

37

wí-yu-xᵘk̓wə́ndə-dá,

If her mother's sister was there,

38

wí·-kuwí-sg̣ú·ya.

she would tell her.

39

wí-i-tłántłq̓á·d(th)ə́-dáʻ,

If her sister-in-law was there,

40

wí-kuwí-sg̣u·ya

she would tell her.

41

wí-x̣-kwi-íl-du·-sg̣ú·ya tłə-dɛ' ɛ́ł·ɛ.

Then it was they who told her father.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
