Crow girl

24 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

máqt'ł gwɛ́is

Crow girl

2

hit'ci-di̯k̓íl·ga tłə-gwɛ́is

The (crow) girl had one child.

3

gu·s-mín-du·-kwi· k̓wə́lxwən yúg̣wa

She gathered mussels all the time.

4

mín·t'ci tsú-gwum-wúl·mít̓aⁱ,

Once she went to get them,

5

wi·-kwi·-gwum-q'míya·t̓uwa,

and she was cooking them,

6

aɴ-gúmɛ́·wi dlú'wiyam,

she did not cease eating,

7

gu·s-dítc hat'ccíl·is hidjú·wi.

everything was so sweet tasting to her.

8

tsú-t'łú·ni,

Now the tide came in,

9

t'łú·ni áiwa gwum-dá-x̣ałtəm.

as the tide came in she was still doing things there.

10

łə́n·wi'yɛ t'łú·ni,

The tide had come in strong,

11

á·iwa-dáʻ-dlú'wiyam.

but she was still eating there.

12

ts̯ú-k̓ła tłə-də̯k̓íl·ga,

Now she thought of her baby,

13

tsú-g̣ɛ́ʻ-yag̣á-da idjáu-tłə-di̯k̓íl·ga

and she ran towards where her baby had been.

14

má·n-x̣-báldi·mis kwi·-hwə́ldiya di̯k̓íl·ga,

But the ocean had already taken away her baby,

15

áɴ-djiʻ kwi·-gálmí·t̓a

she could not get it back again

16

tsú-áx̣·ats,

Then she wept,

17

g̣ałə́l·ma,

Many more,

18

g̣ałə́l·ma ɛhí·mɛ,

many more babies,

19

ɛhí·mɛ,

babies,

20

má-hántł dá·s kwə-hí·mɛ.

there will be more such babies.

21

wɛ́n-áx̣·ats tłə-gwɛ́is.

This is the way the girl cried.

22

tłə-kwí-tłx̯ú tłə-'ni̯k̓ílga

It was my baby that drifted away.

23

x̣-xɛ́n áx̣·ats íʻcdjúʻ tłə-di̯k̓íl·ga.

That is the way she cried when the waves caused her baby to float away.

24

tsú-tsi·-wɛ́·s.

Now so much of that.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
