A new shaman offered to doctor without pay

7 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tlə-'nəx-g̣wə́ns x̣-wɛ́·n-wi-íl·du·n.

Linked dictionary entries (2)
