Dove Myth

12 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

xáyu·nti bá·saq̓

Dove myth

Linked dictionary entry (1)

xáyu·nti dove

2

łá·-də́-k̓aʻ má·-t'łdáyasədjə,

People went to another place (village),

Linked dictionary entries (3)

k̯̓a' people, person · ma another, but, even if, really · t'łdá'yas land, village

3

wí·-náuhi'ná·nu łá·-də́-k̓aʻ gu·s-djí-alicá·-ni'wa·s.

the people went to play shinny and all sorts of games.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

dji come, arrive, with an< it is what/why · gú··s all, always · k̯̓a' people, person · nauhini shinny game

4

wí·-tłə-dí·lúł hís-hidjíʻ-łá.

The young man (dove) went too.

Linked dictionary entries (2)

hidji along, also, too; himself, herself, too, also, along · his along, also, too

5

t̓í·cdji-g̣aháis hɛ·yɛ́·nu-də́-k̓aʻ.

Ten days the people gambled.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

g̣áháis night, or nights · hɛ·yɛ́·nu consort, gamble · k̯̓a' people, person · ticdji ten

6

wí-hidji-dlúqʷsəm tłətc-dí·łúł tłi-iłdłɛ́·g̣ɛg̣ɛdjɛ tłi-iłdit'sí·yɛldjə tłi-ił-du-dlɛ́·-g̣ɛg̣ɛtc.

Now dilu himself was going to sit down where they were sitting w/ their hand game sticks where they sat.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

dilul young man, boy · dlɛgɛg sat/ lived · dluqwsam arise/ get up · tlatc marker for either younger or older people

7

hɛ́i-má·tsi k̓wɛ́·n-djindjí·nu kwítc-dí·lúł.

Now then they brought news to that young man.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

hɛi intensifier · kwatc what/ emphasizes subject, demonstrative · kwɛnɛ took back, returned, brought with, learned · má·tsi just, merely

8

ɛ́·wi-ni̯k̓cúwɛ.

Stop gambling.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

ɛ́·wi quit, stop

9

q̓áyau-tłə-nə'úmná·t'łətc.

Your grandmother died.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

qayau dead

10

án-wántł-ɛ́·wi.

I will not stop.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

ɛ́·wi quit, stop

11

hadáł·a-di'idzí·məs ya-hántł-dú-łən̯ík̓-t'clú'utc,

At a certain time every year when the grass will be getting dry,

Linked dictionary entries (4)

hadáł·a at a certain · hántł he or she will · t'c'lá·t dry · ya irrealis, used during the conditionel

12

t̓ə́ma-wantł-du-áx̣·ats gál·ada-wántł-du tłə-'nə'úmná·-t'łətc.

at that time I will cry and mourn for my grandmother.

Linked dictionary entries (2)

g̣al·áda cry · tama marks subsequent time
