Dove Myth

12 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

xáyu·nti bá·saq̓

Dove myth

2

łá·-də́-k̓aʻ má·-t'łdáyasədjə,

People went to another place (village),

3

wí·-náuhi'ná·nu łá·-də́-k̓aʻ gu·s-djí-alicá·-ni'wa·s.

the people went to play shinny and all sorts of games.

4

wí·-tłə-dí·lúł hís-hidjíʻ-łá.

The young man (dove) went too.

5

t̓í·cdji-g̣aháis hɛ·yɛ́·nu-də́-k̓aʻ.

Ten days the people gambled.

6

wí-hidji-dlúqʷsəm tłətc-dí·łúł tłi-iłdłɛ́·g̣ɛg̣ɛdjɛ tłi-iłdit'sí·yɛldjə tłi-ił-du-dlɛ́·-g̣ɛg̣ɛtc.

Now dilu himself was going to sit down where they were sitting w/ their hand game sticks where they sat.

7

hɛ́i-má·tsi k̓wɛ́·n-djindjí·nu kwítc-dí·lúł.

Now then they brought news to that young man.

8

ɛ́·wi-ni̯k̓cúwɛ.

Stop gambling.

9

q̓áyau-tłə-nə'úmná·t'łətc.

Your grandmother died.

10

án-wántł-ɛ́·wi.

I will not stop.

11

hadáł·a-di'idzí·məs ya-hántł-dú-łən̯ík̓-t'clú'utc,

At a certain time every year when the grass will be getting dry,

12

t̓ə́ma-wantł-du-áx̣·ats gál·ada-wántł-du tłə-'nə'úmná·-t'łətc.

at that time I will cry and mourn for my grandmother.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
