Angel-bird's' eye

12 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

yá·gałs-dəx̣wálx̣wal łu·-dú-git̯k̓íl·at,

They find an angel-bird's eye,

Linked dictionary entries (3)

gitskiat find found · walxwal eye · yá·gałs angel bird, large hawk

2

wí-i-du-y ɛ́·tsədjɛ tú·ya máiqłəm.

when it drops onto a house during the night.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

tu·'ya lowered, fell, sank

3

mái-hán·dliya ma·íł-du̯--k̓íldⁿwa i-ił-du·-qa'wá·ya tú·ya.

No matter how dark the night they find it when they hear it drop.

Linked dictionary entries (6)

kildwa find · hɛndlis dark · íł they · qa'wá·'ya hear · ma another, but, even if, really · tu·'ya lowered, fell, sank

4

wí-á·yu-ił-du̯-k̓íldⁿwa.

They do indeed find it.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

kildwa find

5

wí-an-íł-du·-didzá·ya.

But they do not bring it inside.

Linked dictionary entries (2)

didzá·ya bring inside · íł they

6

mátsi-ił-du·-má·iɣi,

They just wrap it up,

Linked dictionary entry (1)

má·tsi just, merely

7

wí-ni̯k̓ín-dəlibíʻ-gɛsədjə íłtbá·ts.

they deposit it in the roots (stump) of a tree.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

n̯ík̓ín stick, wood

8

wí-wɛ́·n-iłlá·t'cíyada misx̯ú·wi.

That is what they call a good-luck charm.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

misxa² good luck charm

9

i-idzú·djɛ ditdjíyu wí-an̯-k̓ɛ́·łɛ-ditc,

If you bring it inside the house that would not be good,

Linked dictionary entries (2)

idzɛ inside · k̯ʼɛ́·ƚɛ very well

10

an·í-ditc-g̣ɛ́ʻg̣álám,

you would not get anything (luck) for it,

11

má·tsi-nəkwí·nɛ'wɛt'ł.

you would only become poor.

Linked dictionary entry (1)

má·tsi just, merely

12

wɛ́·n-ditc tłə-du kwí· qánu sdlɛ́·nɛn tłi·iłdəmisx̯ú·wi

That is why they hide that good-luck-charm outside.

Linked dictionary entries (4)

kwi demonstrative pronoun · qán·u outside · sadlan to hide · sdƚá·qʼ to wash, bathe
