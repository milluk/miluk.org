The father's helpers, the storks

19 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

dú·lak· tłə-hɛ́-niyɛ k̓áʻ-dəmá·ni'yá·s kwí--didzí-yát'si.

long ago ( long ago ) the storks worked for the people's father.

Linked dictionary entries (3)

dulak stork · k̯̓a' people, person · kwi demonstrative pronoun
