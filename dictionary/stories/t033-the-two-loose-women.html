the two loose women

145 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

ɣayá·ł hú·m̯ɛ́·k̓ɛ

The two loose women

2

wí·-x̣-g̣ug̣wə́s-kwí--dzən'wíyam,

They came from the Lower Coquille River country,

3

wí·-x̣-haltc-kwi·-djíya'nə́ma,

they came canoeing up the creek (from the slough),

4

wí·-tlgú·s-wudlú·dlɛq̓hɛ́'mətc,

they portaged the canoe

5

dá-itcwut'łáya-itcdətłgú·s

there they portaged their canoe.

6

wí·-tłə-tcíhá·'adja g̣ɛ́-itchálts,

Now they went up North Slough (Coos Bay),

7

wí·-x̣g̣ɛ́t-itcwút'łi tsgɛ́·idjə.

and they portaged from there over to Ten Mile Lake.

8

hɛ-itc k̯ibíndji-itcdji.

Now they reached somebody's place (there).

9

wɛ́n-itcdə'lúwɛ kwí·-tłə-hɛthɛ́·dɛ,

And they thought he was the wealthy head man there,

10

tsú-itc-g̣ɛ́ʻ-dɛ́·djɛ tłi-yɛ́·tsədjə

so they went into the house at that place.

11

hɛi-má·tsi-di·'lút'ł-dlú·g̣wa

There was just a boy seated (inside).

12

tsú-itcmánt'ct,

Now they asked him,

13

ámi-k̓áʻ-dəg̣aháis-í

Is there nobody whatever (here)?

14

ə́··. ax̯íyax̯i-tcin díu-dlú·g̣wa

əə/ə (yes). My uncle (mother's brother) lives here.

15

pg̣ális-ɛ́hɛ g̣ɛ́lyɛq-dəpg̣ális.

He has gone salmon fishing.

16

ə́ má··-gwa-án-k̓áʻ

ə/ he does not look at all like a person.

17

g̣ɛ́m-itcníya,

They gave him camas,

18

wí-g̣adjyá-nas-tła-łdjá tłɛ-g̣ɛ́m hɛ́i-kwətc-di·'lút'ł.

but the boy ate only half the camas.

19

ámi-há·p̓-dág̣aháis-í

Is there not water at all here?

20

áⁿ··̓ nəhá·p̓á'yukʷ.

áⁿa (yes) there is some water.

21

łu-wántł-łá·dza.

I will go get it.

22

án-tłɛ́-kwí·-wɛ́·st,

He did not return quickly,

23

tsú-wálwi-tłə-x̣-gwɛ́is,

so then a girl,

24

hít'ci-tłə-x̣-gwɛ́is

one of the girls,

25

hɛi-má·tsi-t'łwɛ́·x̣ɛdjɛ kwí·-dzəg̣wá·qʷ-tłə-dəp̓ílkʷ.

went looking him and then his penis was stuck in an alder.

26

tsú-bi·nát's-tłə-gwɛ́is.

Now the girl went back.

27

ú· áyu·-x̯-án-k̓áʻ

uu/ really he is not a person at all

28

má·tsi-ná· t'łwɛ́·x̣-kwí·-dzəg̣wá·qʷ,

it is just speared into alder,

29

lá·mak̯ x̯-tłə-dəp̓ílkʷ tłətc-dí'lut'ł.

that boy's penis is just a bone.

30

ú· wɛ́·st-hántł kwə-da'ax̯íyax̯itc.

uu/ his uncle (mother's brother) will come home.

31

k̯łáwis-hantł tłə-də'ax̯íyax̯itc

We will see his uncle.

32

kwí·-da-kwə-hɛthɛ́·dɛ.

He must be the wealthy head man.

33

hɛi-má·tsi-gwa·n ɛ́nt'c idj ɛ́' dɛ́·məł dɛ́·djɛ.

And then just a short (fat) ugly man entered.

34

kwí·-x̯-kwíya tłə-də-hɛthɛ́·dɛ.

I guess he must be the wealthy head man.

35

tsú-wɛ́·st.

Now he had returned.

36

tłgú·sidjə ícłá'yáq̓ai.

Go to the canoe.

37

dá-'nəg̣ɛ́lyɛq x̯t'łɛ́·wɛu.

I have salmon in it there.

38

áyu-lá'áyam tłə-gwɛ́isəmɛ

Indeed the girls went.

39

án-ítc-tłgú·s̯-k̓íl·dwa,

They did not find the canoe,

40

án-idjáu-ditc-ídjɛ

there was nothing anywhere.

41

ni̯k̓ín-tła-da-tsí·mq̓,

Only logs lay there,

42

wɛ́n-má·tsi-xú·nəs-x̯iní·m,

and just xuu/nəs grass lay on top of them,

43

wɛ́n-k̓wɛhɛ́i-dɛkʷsí q̓x̣á·yau kwi·-tła-dáʻ-x̯iní·m.

and cut willow limbs lay on top too.

44

tsú-wusəsíyam yɛ́·dzədjə.

So then they went back to the house.

45

má·'na-án-idjáu-ditc̯-k̓íl·dwa.

We did not find anything anywhere at all.

46

hú hɛi-gwá-an-djənɛ́·wɛt'ł i-na-án-ditc̯-k̓íl·dwa?.

Hu/ now is not that strange that you did not find anything?

47

tsú-hidji-tɛ́·ix̣ɛu,

Then he went down to the water himself,

48

hɛ́i-má·tsi-kwi·-wá·sdədza·t̓a tłəł-tłə-xú·nəs wɛ́n-tłə-ɛ́k̓ʷli-kwsí tłə-tłgwí-də́kwsi.

and he brought back the xuu/nəs grass and the little willow limbs.

49

hə́ kwí--'nəq̓wəníyau wí-míts'u.

Hə/ that is my food and I get fat on it.

50

x̣-wɛ́n-íl·at tłətc-tú'mət'ł.

That is what the old man said.

51

tsú-g̣ɛ́ł-əm tsú-łá-tłətc-tú'mət'ł.

Then the next morning that old man went away.

52

ła·-wántł.

I am going to go.

53

dɛ́u

Nephew

54

łú·dədɛ·-də-tłə-húmɛ̯·k̓ɛ

watch these women

55

ínantł án-łu·dəda-ya'áma-ku-húmɛ̯·k̓ɛ.

If you do not watch these women (I will punish you).

56

tsú-hú-wɛ ɛ́tsəm tsu-itcła'áyam.

Now they made preparations and then they went away.

57

án-da-x̯-kúwi kwə-dɛ́·məł isdú·ha'ya.

He cannot be the husband we want.

58

án-ítc-hɛ́·niyɛ-ła'áyam tsi-itc-məqmí-k̯łá·wi.

They had not gone far when they saw an inferior type house

59

oo/. where are you going?

60

ámi-is-ditc-í

Do you not have something?

61

halkʷdí·m

Give me something

62

áyu-itchalkʷdíya,

63

64

65

gú·s-wɛ́·n-tłi-iłnɛhɛ́mq̓ɛtc.

and we are all of the same sort of appearance.

66

gu·s-idjáu-hit'ci-dlú·g̣wa.

67

68

69

hɛ́··i g̣ɛ́ndji-mɛ́·t'cis

70

71

72

74

75
