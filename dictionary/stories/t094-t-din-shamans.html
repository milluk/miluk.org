ᴍít̓î·din shamans

23 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

tłə-mit̓ɛ́·din-k̓a i-du·-tsłú·nam,

When people paid a mit'ee/din (shaman),

2

wí-á·yú·-du wi-x̣-wɛ́n-íl·du·n.

indeed this is what he said.

3

djí·-hantł-n̥dú·ha'ya,

However you desire it,

4

x̣-wɛ́·n-wantł-wá·wa.

that is the way it will be done.

5

inantł-dú·ha'ya yaq̓áim-x̣walx̣wal,

If you wish him blind eyed (blinded),

6

wí-ma-hantł-x̣-wɛ́·n.

that is how it will be.

7

indú·ha'ya djíl·ɛdə-áɴyá-wɛn,

If you want his legs to become no good,

8

wí-ma-hántł-x̣-wɛn.

that is how it will be.

9

indú·ha'ya qłá·də́-kaya'li,

If you want his feet to rot,

10

wí-ma-hantł-x̣-wɛn.

that is how it will be.

11

i-t'łix̯índɛ ány̯á-k̓ilɛ,

If his arms to be no good,

12

wí-his-hántł-x̣-wɛ́n.

that will be done too.

13

wí-indú·ha'ya ɛ́·ci'níyɛ,

And if you want him to become a forest being (î:<cin),

14

wí-ma-hántł-x̣-wɛn.

that is how it will be.

15

wí-indú·-ha'ya k̓xá·-də́t̓ɛ,

And if you want him to shoot himself,

16

wí-ma-hántł-dú-x̣-wɛn.

that is how it will be.

17

indú·ha'ya q̓álaⁿ,

If you want him to injure himself,

18

wí-his-hántł-x̣-wɛ́n

that will be done also.

19

djíʻ-n̥dú·ha'ya,

Whatever you desire,

20

x̣-wɛ́·n-wantł-wá·tsan.

that is how I will do it.

21

á·yu-du-mɛ́-x̣-wɛ́n.

And indeed it would be done that way.

22

wɛ́·n-tłə-mit̓ɛ́·din áɴ-wɛ́·n-ditc.

That is why (mit!î:<din) shamans were no good (evil).

23

hɛ́lɛ'yu-áɴ-wɛ́n x̣-íl·áx̣q̓ai'nitc.

They were even worse than (ilx-q!a<in) shamans.

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
