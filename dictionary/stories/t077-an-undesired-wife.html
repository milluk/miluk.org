An undesired wife

27 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

i-du-tłɛ-hɛthɛ́·dɛ k̓áʻ-d(th)əhí·mɛ,

If (he was) a very rich person's child,

2

wi-ya-áɴ-dú·ha'ya tłə-dəhú·mis,

even if he did not want his wife,

3

wí-ma-dú-daʻ-dlú·g̣wa tłə-dəmá·ni'yá·sətc,

she remained there nevertheless with his parents,

4

tłə-dədɛ́·mił-dəmá·ni'yas.

her husband's parents.

5

wí-má·tsi du-má-hu·mis-g̣álam.

He merely got another wife.

6

wí·-wɛ́·n-du-sín·sá·nu kʷdlí·yɛn tłə-hú·mis.

They called that woman an undesired one.

7

wí-yú-wút's tłɛ-x̣-dɛ́·mił-dəhɛ́t'łɛ,

Maybe it was her husband's older brother,

8

kwi·-dú-níya tłə-dəhɛ́t'łɛ,

she would be given to his older brother,

9

yi-x̣-kuwí dú·há'ya.

if he desired her.

10

wí-yuwút's tłə-dəbú·yɛ di̯k̓ílga,

Or maybe the boy's mother's brother,

11

yúwút's kwí·-níya.

maybe he would give her to him.

12

yɛ-áɴ-dú·há'ya tłə-hú·mis,

If he did not want the woman,

13

wí-yúwut's wí·-ya-g̣á·łə́lya d(th)əhúmɛ̯·k̓ɛ,

maybe when he had so (very) many wives,

14

wí-yuwút's máʻ-daʻ-dlú·-g̣wa.

she might remain there nevertheless.

15

yɛ-x̣-má·ni'yá·sdə yuwút's bíya-tłə-də̯k̓ílga,

If her parents perhaps took back home their child,

16

wí-hɛthɛ́·dɛ-dihí·mɛ,

if a child of very rich people,

17

x̯-wɛ n-iłwá·tsan,

if that is what they did,

18

wí-yuwu't's kwi-yɛ́·nu wúlmɛⁿ.

then sometimes they might fight over it.

19

wí-yuwút's kwi-yímł-qdlaⁱ k̯̓í·dzímɛⁿ.

They might kill one another because of that.

20

x̣-wɛ́·n ditc-tłí-ił-áɴ k̓áʻ-dihí·mɛ wɛ́·n-hú·da·t,

That is why they never made mock thus of the person's child,

21

náim-ił-'ɛ́lqs.

because they feared that.

22

máʻ-ił-áɴ-dú·ha'ya máʻ-iłd1ə́g̣wá·q.

Even if they'dn't want her they had her remain nevertheless.

23

tsí-á ɴ-gu·s-mín-g̣ɛ́'-tsú·-tsuwaⁱ,

But he might not sleep with her there all the time,

24

áɴ-dji-yu-kwí·-kʷdlí·nan,

he was not enamored of her,

25

náim̯-k̓ílɛ k̓áʻ-di̯k̓ílga,

but since she was a child of good (well-to-do) people,

26

x̣-wɛ́·n-ditc tłi-ił-kwí an-hú·da·t.

that is why they did not mock her

27

ɛ́lqs'ił yuwút's-kwi-yɛ́·nu tsú·dzə́mɛᵘ.

They're afraid perhaps they be killing one another because
