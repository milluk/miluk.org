A shaman's increase of power dance

6 lines · from Melville Jacobs’s Coos texts, dictated by Annie Miner Peterson (1933–34); keyed 1986–1990.

1

i-du-g̣ałə́l·ya tła·-də́g̣wəns wí-hɛłt̓-ha-tə́m·iłdíyɛ,

When her dream becomes too strong for her,

2

wí-ya-áɴ-gisgínt'łai wí-x̣ɛ́nx̣ɛnu.

if she does not tie it together she becomes ill.

3

wí-q̓áyau-du-ya-áɴ-x̣-má·-íl·áx̣q̓ain náut.

If the other shaman does not put it for her she may die.

4

wɛ́·n-ditc tłə-du-kwi·-dá·s-gisgínt'łai.

That is why she ties it together (dances) again.

5

x̣-wɛ́·n-ditc-du tłə-kwi·-dá·s-yisyɛ́ls tłə-íl·áx̣q̓ain,

That's reason why the shaman has power increase dance again,

6

náim álqsa.

because she is afraid (of becoming ill and dying).

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
