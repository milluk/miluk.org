In the texts

háma'wə́-x̣-tłə-k̓áʻ du-wú-l·a'waʻ.

‘I think I will go seek people.’

That whittles-his-penis old man or The five brothers, line 10

1990 source file: H2 · id: e0349-hama-we

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
