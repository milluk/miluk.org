In the texts

wí-dáʻ-dzə́g̣ɛ́·q̓ x̣ínxin-dək̓ú·máx·idja,

‘She hung there from the saddle horn,’

Hadjíyasa, tłɛ́iɣəl, and others, line 16

wɛ́n-tgidzín'itc-də-dzəg̣ág̣a

‘And their granddaughter was hanging up.’

The old couple and their-grandson became ashamed there, line 6

wí-dáʻ-du-dzəg̣ág̣a tłə́-kʷsíyu.

‘and there it hung from the limb of the tree.’

Baby's cradle was hung, line 14

qʻdíl-q̓ham-tsantł kwə-łɛ́·ł kwá·ntł kwí· x̣ug̣ú-dzag̣ág̣a.

‘They say they will try to shoot down that medicine which will be hanging from above’

There were many people at that place, line 14

łú·-tsántł-dzag̣ág̣aʻ x̣-g̣aháisitc.

‘They say it will be hanging down from the (sky) above.’

There were many people at that place, line 8

tsú-ił-kwi·-qʻə́t'sa tłətc-k̓ʷhán·asətc dzə-g̣ág̣a,

‘Then they cut off what was hanging from his ear,’

He eats human children, line 89
