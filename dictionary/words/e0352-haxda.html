háx̣da

drag away

Attested forms

  • háx̣da corroborated

In the texts

an-íł-du·-wɛ́·n-g̣alam má·tsi-ił-du-g̯ɛ-háx̣da.

‘they just dragged her along to there.’

People of the poorer class, line 27

wí-x̣-wɛ́·n-ditc-du tłi-ił-kwí·-háx̣da.

‘That is why they dragged them.’

People of the poorer class, line 40

1990 source file: H2 · id: e0352-haxda

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
