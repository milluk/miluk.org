xukwɛl

wooden red cedar bucket

Attested forms

  • xᵘkwɛ́l unverified
  • xʷkwɛ́·l corroborated

In the texts

wí·-kwi·-tłə-hít'ci tlə-íl·áxq̓ain kwí·-kwi·-g̣álám tłu-xʷkwɛ́·l,

‘One (male Alsea) shaman took ( picked up the bucket) the bucket,’

An Alsea shaman was forced to take back a pain-power, line 5

1990 source file: XUKWEL · id: e1056-xukwel

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
