garment

In the texts

ámi-du·-wɛ́t'ł dza'án·á'nu.

‘without clothes on she lay.’

The trickster person who made the country, line 1140

mái-hantł-du ámi-wɛ́t'ł áɴ-hu·t'sú·təm,

‘Even though she be without clothes and act wrongly,’

The trickster person who made the country, line 1151

ámi-u-wɛ́t'ł titsú·n,

‘He threw me down without my garments (covering me),’

A husband must not see his wife nude, line 14

1990 source file: WETL · id: e1004-wetl

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
