luwakw

lightning

Attested forms

  • lúwá·kʷ unverified

In the texts

hɛⁱ-dú-á·yú'itc hidjú·wi łú-tú·ya tłə-lúwa·qʷ,

1990 source file: LUWAKW · id: e0583-luwakw

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
