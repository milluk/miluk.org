Words · H

haihaya

to have an affair

Attested forms

  • haya'ná·ya corroborated
  • hɛ́ihɛyɛ'nu corpus

In the texts

gu·s-míɴ húm̯ɛ́·k̓ɛ há·ihaya'ná·ya.

‘he consorted with (other) women all the time.’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 103

wí-yə-hɛ́·wi wí·-kwi·-tłə-tcú·wáʻtcəs kwí·yɛ də-háihaya'ná·ya tłɛ-x̣- djí·xwə́nt̓ɛ.

‘When he had grown up djíxwɛ́nt̓î began to have an affair with Popeyes’

Djiixwɛ́nt'î, line 22

wɛ́n-kwi hái-haya'ná·ya.

‘and he (my husband) was having an affair with her.’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 149

wí-má·tsi-yá-gum hɛ́ihɛyɛ'nu tłə-'n̥dɛ́·mił.

‘Now my husband began to consort with (other) women.’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 137

wɛ́n-má·tsiya-hɛ́ihɛyɛ'nu tłə-dədɛ́·mił.

‘Then her husband began to consort sexually’

Kitty Hayes, line 11

1990 source file: HAIHAYA · id: e0388-haihaya

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
