hɛn̯í·k̓is-x̣-wɛ́·n djí-tłə-n̥tsłɛ́·nɛ́·-k̓aʻ yáit'ct-diłt̓áyaⁿ.

‘It was just like the way the moving people (the whites) erased their marking (obtained divorce papers).’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 18

hɛn̯í·k̓is'u gwa-g̣adlá·na k̓a'atc-dlú·g̣wa.

‘just as if I were living with the people of down below’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 339

tłə-dədɛ́·mił-dəmá·ni'yas kwí·yɛ́·-du hɛn̯í·k̓is hidjím·ił-má·ni'ya·s,

‘Her husband's parents were like her own (parents) parents henceforth,’

A wife and children belonged to, line 4
