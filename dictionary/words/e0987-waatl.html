wá'at'ł

spider

Attested forms

  • wá'at'ł corroborated
  • wawá'at'ł corroborated

In the texts

'ə́̓ tsú-dá-x̣-gúm x̣t̓á·-dík̯tsa·s tłɛ-x̣-wá·-wá'at'ł,

‘Oh! I guess the giant spider is throwing (shoveling) out his ashes,’

Snow is the ashes from the hearth of the giant spider, line 2

tsú-da-x̯-gum t'łcá·-dík̯tsa·s tłɛ-x̣-wá·-wá'at'ł.

‘I suppose giant spider is sweeping out his ashes.’

Snow is the ashes from the hearth of the giant spider, line 5

also cited (form not located verbatim in the line): The trickster person who made the country 1796

1990 source file: WAATL · id: e0987-waatl

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
