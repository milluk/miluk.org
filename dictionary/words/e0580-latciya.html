latciya

to name, to call

Attested forms

  • lat'cí·dai corroborated
  • lat'cíya·da corroborated
  • lɛ·t'cí·m corroborated

In the texts

wí-ɛ́n·ɛ wí·-kwɛx̣tc-k̓áʻ wɛ́·n-lat'cíya·da yú·mi łímda·was,

‘I will have the star (that one) that the people call the hunters,’

The girls who wished to have stars for their husbands, line 9

wɛ́n·antł-du-lat'cí·dai,

‘You are to name me,’

The trickster person who made the country, line 116

wɛ́·n-tcil-h ntł-du-lɛ·t'cí·m má·tłi̯k̓.

‘and so they will name you snail.’

The trickster person who made the country, line 367

kwítc-x̣-wɛ́n-lɛ·t'cí·m-k'áʻ hɛt-hɛ́·dɛ,
