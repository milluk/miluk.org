In the texts

ə́· k̓á·dic kwi·-t'sg̣á·ʻ dix̣ú·t'ł.

‘ə-! An odorous ant bit his penis.’

The trickster person who made the country, line 290

1990 source file: D2 · id: e0069-dixutl

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
