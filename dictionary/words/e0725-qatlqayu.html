Words · Q

qatlqayu

the hammer, maul

Attested forms

  • qá·tłqáyu corroborated
  • dəqá·tłqaⁱ corroborated
  • dəqátłqai corroborated

In the texts

tsú-kwi'yú-tsg̣waʻ tłə-dəx̣-qá·tłqáyu,

‘and he struck with the hammer (maul)’

The trickster person who made the country, line 1614

tsú-t'syám·i'ya tłɛ-x̣-dí·łúł tłə-dəqátłqai.

‘So then the young man dropped the hammer.’

The trickster person who made the country, line 1619

also cited (form not located verbatim in the line): The trickster person who made the country 1624

1990 source file: QATLQAYU · id: e0725-qatlqayu

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
