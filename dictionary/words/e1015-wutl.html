Words · W

wutl

step over, climb, get over

Attested forms

  • wút'łi corroborated
  • wút'łu corroborated

In the texts

nik̓·ínu·-wút'łi,

‘she stepped over a log,’

A woman obtained fir power and learned two medicines, line 3

hámáq̓ án-dji-wút'łu ni̯k̓ínu.

‘He saw it could not get over a log.’

The person that halloos, line 12

wí·-x̣-wɛ́n-hɛ́·yɛ náim-du-án-dji-wút'łi.

‘And that's how he'd get a distance ahead it could not jump’

The person that halloos, line 13

wudlám·aq-n̯ík̓in an-hántł-du·-djíʻ-da-wút'łi.

‘She could not climb over the large logs.’

The trickster person who made the country, line 508

1990 source file: WUTL · id: e1015-wutl

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
