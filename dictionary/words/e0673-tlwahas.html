tlwahas

run away

Attested forms

  • tłwá·hás corroborated
  • tłwáhasá·nu corroborated
  • dutłwá·hasá·nu corroborated

In the texts

wí-áɴ-dú·ha'ya tłɛ-x̣-ɴancy wí-gu·s-mín kwi·-tłwáhasá·nu.

‘But Nancy did not want him. She ran away from him continually.’

Cissy, line 11

wí·-kwi·-du tłwá·hás-wi-du,

‘So then I ran away,’

Qɛ́icɛc, Batter, told about her own life in these words, line 5

wí·-gu·s-mín-dutłwá·hasá·nu,

‘She ran away continually,’

Hadjíyasa, tłɛ́iɣəl, and others, line 10

1990 source file: TLWAHAS · id: e0673-tlwahas

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
