huwɛ

the reason

Attested forms

  • húwɛ corroborated
  • huwɛ corroborated

In the texts

áɴ-huwɛ tłi-ił-kwí·-áɴ-dú·ha'ya tłə-t'sə́n·á·-g̣wəns náim-iłmaq̓al·is·

‘They did not want a thunder dream (power) because it was jealous.’

Getting rid of a thunder dream-power, line 12

‘We can not help it that we are alone.’

A young man grew up alone, and then he split himself, line 11

‘They were so very much afraid of that,’

The care of nursing babies, line 37

‘The reason they knew what was the sickness of a (childhood) child,’

The care of nursing babies, line 51

an-dú-huwɛ tłi·ił-du--kwí k'ált tłi̯-k̓ílga-dədɛ́wɛⁱ ina·án-kwi--k'ált,

‘The reason they hallooed to the baby's cry was that if it was not called to,’

People called to baby's cry to come along, line 18

aɴ-dú-huwɛ tłə-kwí·'nɛ'wɛ́t'ł k̓áʻ-dihí·mɛ tłə-du-kwí-án- x̣-wi-hámaq̓áma náim-ił-áɴ-dú·há'ya tłə-dɛx̣-má·ni'yas.

‘the reason the children of poor persons were never seen was because their parents did not want it ( them to be seen by richer people).’

Poor people did not want, line 2

an-dú-húwɛ tłə-kwí·-wɛ́·n'nə́mɛ,

‘That is why they were like that,’

Poor people did not want, line 26

án-du-huwɛ tlə-kwi-áɴ-du-ha'ya hɛthɛ́·dɛ-k̓áʻ náim-tłə-dɛx̣-má·-humɛ̯·-k̓ɛ maq̓ályá'ił-du,

‘She did not want a very rich person because his other wives would be jealous of her,’

People of the poorer class, line 41
