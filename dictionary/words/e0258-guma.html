gúma

with his it seems to be even

Attested forms

  • gúma corroborated

In the texts

łín·wi-maq̓ál·is hís-gúma-aɴ dú·há'ya kwí·-níhi·mɛdɛ.

‘it was so extremely jealous that it did not even want them to have children.’

Getting rid of a thunder dream-power, line 18

hís-gúma-áɴ-lix̯lí·x̯,

‘it never even moved at all,’

The pouty children, line 39

wí-in-dɛ́·míłɛ wí-his-gúma tłə-dɛx̣-dɛ́·mił án-hámaq̓ tłə-dəmáʻ-ditc.

‘Even her very husband may not look at her something (genitals).’

A husband must not see his wife nude, line 5

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
