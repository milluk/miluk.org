su'wáini

drew; puckered; grimaced

Attested forms

  • su'wáini corroborated

In the texts

wɛ́·n-ditc-du tłə-du-kwi-su'wáini dɛhɛ́ʟ tłɛ-x̣-q̓á·ná-k̓·ílga.

‘That is why the very young infant drew (puckered, grimaced) its face.’

Grandmother afterbirth, line 3

1990 source file: S · id: e0763-su-waini

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
