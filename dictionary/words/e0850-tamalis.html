custom, ways

In the texts

tłi-iłdɛ́·x̣-ta·má·łidjis má·tsiya íł-x̣-wɛn dətá·má·łis djíʻtłə-n̥tsłɛ́·nɛ́·-k̓a-də-tá·má·łis,

‘Their ways became the ways ( the customs ) of the moving people (the whites),’

Head wives, line 31

1990 source file: TAMALIS · id: e0850-tamalis

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
