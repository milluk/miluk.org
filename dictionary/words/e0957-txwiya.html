txwiya

send (a person somewhere)

Attested forms

  • tx̣wudzá·t̓a corroborated
  • txwíya corroborated
  • wutxwíya corpus

In the texts

wí-tsu-t̓ə́ma g̣ɛ́ʻ-k̓áʻ-txwíya tłə-dədɛ́·miłidjɛ,

‘then they would send a person yonder to her husband,’

A rejected wife and her, line 39

wí-x̣-wɛ́·n káʻ-tx̣wudzá·t̓a,

‘he would send a person in this manner,’

Poor people who became wealthy, line 10

x̣úg̣u wutxwíya

‘They sent me from up above.’

The pouty children, line 51

1990 source file: TXWIYA · id: e0957-txwiya

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
