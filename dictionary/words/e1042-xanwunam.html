xanwunam

was angry

Attested forms

  • x̣án'wúnam corroborated
  • x̣an'únam corroborated
  • x̣an corroborated

In the texts

wí·-kwi·-x̣an'únam tłi-ił-kwí-aɴ-sg̣ú·ya.

‘and he was angry that they had not told him.’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 28

łín·wí-kwi·-x̣án'wúnam tłə-kwi-hɛ́wɛ·s·-g̣wə́sg̣wai tłə-dədɛ́·mił.

‘He took it so hard (got so angry) because she had told such a lie to her husband.’

Kitty Hayes, line 32

wí·-tlə-'nəx̣-ɛ́nɛ wí-his-hídji x̣an-wúnam,

‘My mother herself was angry also,’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 29

1990 source file: XANWUNAM · id: e1042-xanwunam

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
