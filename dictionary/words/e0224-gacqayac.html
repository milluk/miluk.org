g̣acq̓á·yac

to bat or strike a ball

Attested forms

  • g̣acq̓á·yac corroborated

In the texts

tsí-u-dú-g̯ɛ-áɴ tsáltts tlɛ-k̓wɛ́·sis yu-wu-du-g̣acq̓á·yac.

‘because I never missed a ( shinny ball ) ball when I struck at it.’

Qɛ́icɛc, Batter, told about her own life in these words, line 51

1990 source file: G2 · id: e0224-gacqayac

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
