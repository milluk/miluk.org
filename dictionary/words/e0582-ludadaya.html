ludadaya

watch and take care of

Attested forms

  • lú·dədá·ya corroborated
  • lú·dədaya corroborated
  • lú·dədími·du·n corroborated
  • lu·dədá·ya corroborated

‘I will watch and take care of my children.’

A male shaman watcher took, line 2
