djal

fight/ take revenge

Attested forms

  • dləƚdʒə́l·at corpus
  • dləldjíldu·nis corroborated
  • dliłdjí·lu corroborated

In the texts

wí-má·tsi-t̓ə́ma tłə-n̥tsłɛ́·nɛ́·-k̓aʻ kwi·-dləldjíldu·nis tłə́-k̓aʻ,

‘At that time the moving people (the whites) fought the people (Indians),’

Cissy, line 12

wí-kwi-yímł-qdlaⁱ du-dliłdjí·lu.

‘then they would have a fight because of it.’

Gossips and liars, line 5

also cited (form not located verbatim in the line): That whittles-his-penis old man or The five brothers 242

1990 source file: DJAL · id: e0136-djal

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
