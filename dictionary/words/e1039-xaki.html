x̣ák̯̓i

gnaw

Attested forms

  • x̣ák̯̓i corroborated
  • x̣ák̯̓i'íya corroborated

In the texts

wɛ́n-má·tsi̯-x̣ák̓i'íya-tłə-dəqłá,

‘Then he just gnawed at his foot,’

The young man stepped on snail's back, line 10

wí-i-kwi̯·-x̣ák̓i wí-wudə́n-yáicdjadə-lá·lu·

‘and while gnawing at it the blood got in his mouth.’

The young man stepped on snail's back, line 11

1990 source file: XAKI · id: e1039-xaki

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
