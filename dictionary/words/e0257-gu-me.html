gú'mɛʻ

name of a particular worm

Attested forms

  • gú'mɛʻ corroborated
  • gu·'mɛ́ʻ corroborated

In the texts

tłətc-gwáʻ-gú'mɛʻ kwi-dáʻ-dłúwíyam tłə-dəg̣ál·a'yu.

‘It is like a gú'mɛʻ that is eating on him there inside him.’

Shamans cured tuberculosis, line 6

tłɛ́-x̣-gwáʻ-gu·'mɛ́ʻ-ditc.

‘that thing like a gú'mɛʻ worm.’

Shamans cured tuberculosis, line 8

1990 source file: G4 · id: e0257-gu-me

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
