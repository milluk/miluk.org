blew away

Attested forms

In the texts

ɛ́··· tłə-dəyɛ́·ts kwí·-p̓icicíyam tłətc-hú·'mi̯k̓,

‘ee/ee the old woman's house blew away,’

The young man became a sea gull, line 32

tsú-his-hidjí-p̓c·íᵘ tłətc-hú·'mi̯k̓

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
