scratched up

In the texts

tłə-'nəqłá gu·s-dú-kwi tłə-'n̥dzɛ́·t'łis gu·s-dú-kwi-haixáhaⁱ.

Annie Miner Peterson, line 208

1990 source file: H2 · id: e0358-haixahai

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
