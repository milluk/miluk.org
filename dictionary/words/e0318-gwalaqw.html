gwalaqw

savings, payment

Attested forms

  • g̣wə́lá·qʷ corroborated
  • g̣wálquwts corroborated
  • g̣wálqʷs corroborated
  • g̣wálqʷts corroborated

In the texts

‘Now he had paid for his wife.’

‘and so (in that way) her husband paid for her.’
