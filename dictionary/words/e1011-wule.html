go along

  • wəłɛ́·ł corroborated

In the texts

wəłɛ́·ł-wantł.

‘I'll go along.’

A girl became a dangerous being of the woods, line 6

á·-yu-wəłɛ·ł.

‘Indeed I'll go along.’

A girl became a dangerous being of the woods, line 7

hís-hidjí-itc-du-wułɛ́·ł tłə-dəhí·-mɛ tłə-gwɛ́is.

‘The girl's two children went along with him.’

Sea Otter Narrative, line 80

wułɛ́-l-wantł ínantł-t̓ə́m·dzawa.

‘I will go along with you when you pack it back.’

Pheasant, line 12

i-nantł-wə́s·i wi-wułɛ́·ł-wántł.

‘When you return (to Siuslau) I will go with you.’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 145

i-hantł-wə́s·i kwə-'nə' ɛ́nɛ wułɛ́·ł-wantł.

‘When my mother goes back I will go with her.’

Annie Miner Peterson, line 151

1990 source file: WULE · id: e1011-wule

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
