lix̯lí·mu

took in/ invite

Attested forms

  • lix̯lí·mu corroborated

In the texts

tsú-du·-ɛ́·wi-də́-k̓aʻ tsú-hantł-du-lix̯lí·mu tłə-g̣áníya·da.

‘And when the people finished (shinny) then they were going to invite into the houses the visitors who had come to play.’

The trickster person who made the country, line 1250

x̣-wɛ́·n má·tsi-g̣atq̓áidiya mán-lix̯lí·mu tłi-hí·mɛ,

‘That's why as evening approached they took their hime then’

Children had to be inside at dusk, line 5

1990 source file: L · id: e0571-lixlimu

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
