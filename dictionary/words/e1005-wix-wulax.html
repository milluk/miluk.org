wix̯'wúl·ax̯

sending and telling

  • wix̯'wúl·ax̯ corroborated
  • u'wix̯wílx̯i·n corroborated

In the texts

máʻ-gwum-má·tsi k̓áʻ-wix̯'wúl·ax̯,

also cited (form not located verbatim in the line): The trickster person who made the country 1516

1990 source file: WIXWULAX · id: e1005-wix-wulax

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
