kwa'ányát'ł

gossip, tell tales, lie

Attested forms

  • kwa'ányát'ł corroborated
  • kwa'anyá·t'ł corroborated

In the texts

wí-á·yú-du ánya-kwa'anyá·t'ł.

‘Then it would tell tales no longer.’

Gossips and liars, line 10

x̯-wɛ́·n il-du-wá·tsan tłi-hí·mɛ yu-kwa'ányát'ł.

‘That is what they did to a child when it gossiped.’

Gossips and liars, line 11

1990 source file: K · id: e0505-kwa-anyat-l

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
