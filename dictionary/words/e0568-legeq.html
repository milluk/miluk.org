red headed woodpecker

In the texts

tsú-á·yu-lɛg̣ɛ́lq-dji.

‘Now red headed woodpecker arrived.’

The trickster person who made the country, line 315

gwa-yú·t'c wantł lɛg̣ɛ́l·ɛq-dəsɛ́ʟ,

‘I will pretend they are red headed woodpecker heads,’

The trickster person who made the country, line 611

mái-lɛg̣ɛ́lq tłi-iłdag̣aháis,

‘If their day was red headed woodpecker,’

Doctoring power appeared like a person, line 8

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
