In the texts

hɛ́i-má·tsi-kwi·-wá·sdədza·t̓a tłəł-tłə-xú·nəs wɛ́n-tłə-ɛ́k̓ʷli-kwsí tłə-tłgwí-də́kwsi.

1990 source file: D · id: e0031-dekwsi

The voice in these texts is Annie Miner Peterson’s, recorded by Melville Jacobs in 1933–34.
