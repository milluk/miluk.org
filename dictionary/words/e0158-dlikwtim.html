dlikwtim

lie down to sleep / sleep

Attested forms

  • dlí·kwtim corpus
  • dlí·gwɛkw corpus
  • dlí·qʷtim corroborated

In the texts

tsú ni̯k̓ín dihá·la'ləsədjə dlí·kʷtim

‘Then they lay down in a hollow tree.’

Black bear and pack basket bear (grizzly), line 98

tsú-gwum-qánu·dja gum-dlí·kʷtim.

‘Now they were going to lie down to sleep outside.’

The girls who wished to have stars for their husbands, line 3

tsú-hantł-x̣ʷdlá·t's tłə-k̓áʻ-x̣ux̣ú·djə dlí·gwɛkʷ

‘Now people were going to exit the sweat house they slept in.’

Dug-out-of-ground child and popped out of fire, line 17

wí·-i-dlí·qʷtim wí·-sədlə́ntc-k̯ím·i.

‘When they went to bed they cried secretly.’

Black bear and pack basket bear (grizzly), line 42
